# Learning what the seller wants

Ask before drafting the offer. Questions for the user to use on their next call or meeting with the owner (not the broker alone, where possible). Ask openly; listen more than talk.

## Questions

**Why and when**
1. What made now the right time to sell?
2. What does your ideal timeline look like? Is there a date that matters (year-end, a move, a health matter, a grandchild)?
3. What will you do after? (Tells you how long they want to stay involved.)

**Money**
4. Is there a number you need to walk away with after debts and taxes? (Don't ask about their taxes in detail; their CPA handles that.)
5. Do you have debts or a line of credit that need to be paid off at closing?
6. How do you feel about being paid part of the price over time?

**People and legacy**
7. Which employees matter most to you, and what would you want for them?
8. Do customers know you personally? How should they hear about a new owner?
9. Should the name, location or anything about how the business is run stay the same?

**Role after closing**
10. How long would you be willing to help with the handover? Full-time, part-time, on call?
11. Are there parts of the business only you know how to do (licenses, estimating, key accounts)?

**Process**
12. Who else is advising you (spouse, CPA, lawyer, broker), and who has the final say?
13. Have you had other offers or a failed sale before? What went wrong?

## Reading the answers

- Talks about employees and customers first → certainty and legacy packages land better than a higher headline.
- Talks about a number first → headline matters; use deferral and earnout to reach it.
- Has a date → speed and certainty are worth something; trade them for price or terms.
- Wants out fast → transition is a risk; price it in, or build a longer paid consulting period.
- A failed sale before → certainty and evidence the buyer can close (financing letters, advisors named) matter.

Record answers in `notes.md` with the date and who said what. They are the seller's statements, not verified facts.
