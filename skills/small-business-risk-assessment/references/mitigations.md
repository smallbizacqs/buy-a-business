# Mitigations to raise

Options to put in front of the user for each high or critical risk. They are tools to discuss with the seller and advisors, not recommendations for this deal. Who decides: price and structure, the user (with the lender, which must accept any seller note terms); contract wording, the lawyer; tax effects, the CPA.

## Where each tool lives

| Tool | Where it goes | Hand off to |
|---|---|---|
| Lower price, or price on current rather than peak earnings | LOI | `small-business-valuation`, `small-business-acquisition-negotiation` |
| Seller note (part of the price paid over time) | LOI, then purchase agreement | `seller-note-structuring`, `small-business-deal-structure` |
| Earnout (part of the price paid only if targets are hit) | LOI, then purchase agreement | `small-business-deal-structure` |
| Transition / consulting period for the owner | LOI, then a consulting agreement | `seller-transition-plan` |
| Diligence condition (deal goes ahead only if X is confirmed) | LOI | `loi-for-small-business-acquisition` |
| Specific diligence requests | `diligence-tracker.csv` | `small-business-financial-due-diligence`, `small-business-operational-due-diligence`, `small-business-legal-due-diligence` |
| Representations, warranties and indemnities | Purchase agreement | `small-business-purchase-agreement` (lawyer drafts) |
| Insurance | Before closing | insurance broker |
| Walk away | `decisions.md` | `acquisition-go-no-go-review` |

Note on SBA loans: SBA rules limit how seller notes, earnouts and seller roles after closing can be structured, and those rules change. Tell the user to check the current SOP with the lender (and `sba-sop-rule-check`) before proposing a structure; don't state the rule from memory.

## By factor

**Customer concentration.** Customer calls before closing (with the seller's permission and usually with the seller present); an earnout or seller note tied to the top account staying; a longer owner transition focused on introducing the buyer to top customers; a diligence condition that key contracts with change-of-control clauses get the customer's consent; pricing on earnings with the riskiest account discounted.

**Supplier concentration.** Supplier consent to assignment as a closing condition; a conversation with the supplier before closing (with permission); identify and price an alternate source during diligence.

**Owner dependence.** A paid transition period with defined duties; documentation of the owner's processes during diligence; hiring or promoting a second in command before or soon after closing (budgeted in earnings); a seller note so the owner keeps a stake in a smooth handover; replacement salary deducted from earnings.

**Key employees.** Retention bonuses (who funds them is negotiable); meeting key staff before closing once the seller agrees; checking for undocumented promises in the seller's disclosures; new employment terms reviewed by a lawyer.

**Licenses held by one person.** Confirm the transfer route with a lawyer and the licensing board before signing the LOI if possible; the user or a staying employee qualifies before closing; the owner stays as qualifier for a defined period under a written agreement; a closing condition that licenses are in place.

**Premises and lease.** Landlord consent to assignment, or a new lease, as a closing condition (`loi-for-small-business-acquisition`); a lease term with buyer-held renewals that covers the loan (ask the lender what it needs); market rent priced into earnings (`sde-and-add-backs-analysis`); with a seller-landlord, a new market-rent lease signed at closing and, if wanted, an option or first right to buy; counsel reviews the assignment and guarantee terms (`small-business-legal-due-diligence`).

**Labor availability.** Budget wage increases in the model; check pricing power; a retention plan; look at apprenticeship or training partnerships after closing.

**PE consolidation.** Factor competitor wage pressure into the labor budget; protect key staff; note the exit upside without paying for it.

**Macro exposure.** Stress test the debt (`sba-dscr-and-affordability`); prefer fixed-rate debt or a larger cash reserve if the user can; a working capital cushion at closing (`working-capital-peg-for-small-business`).

**Distressed / turnaround.** Price on current earnings; heavier seller financing or an earnout so the seller shares the recovery risk; a larger reserve; walk away if the lender can't underwrite on historical results.

**Hidden upside.** Keep it out of the price. Put it in the 100-day plan (`first-100-days-after-acquisition`).
