# Public-record searches the buyer can run

Searching public websites is not contacting anyone, but run searches only with the user's OK and log every one. A search that isn't logged didn't happen. Results are leads for counsel, not conclusions; names match the wrong people all the time.

## Searches

| Search | Where (categories, not vendors) | Search for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| State business registration | Secretary of State business entity search for the state of formation and each operating state | Entity legal name, DBAs | Status, formation date, registered agent, officers |
| UCC filings | Secretary of State UCC search | Entity name, owner names | Secured lenders and equipment financers; compare with the debt schedule |
| Tax liens and judgments | County recorder or clerk; state tax authority lien lists where published | Entity and owners | Federal and state tax liens, judgment liens |
| State court cases | State or county court online docket | Entity, DBAs, owners | Civil suits, collections, contract disputes |
| Federal court cases | Federal court electronic records system | Entity and owners | Federal suits, bankruptcy filings |
| Licensing boards | The board for each license in the inventory | License numbers, holder names | Status, expiry, discipline |
| Trademarks | National trademark office search | Business name and logo words | Whether the name is registered, by whom, and conflicts |
| Domains | Public registration lookup | Each domain | Registrar, registration dates; registrant often private |
| Consumer complaints | State attorney general complaint data where published; public review sites | Business name | Patterns, not single complaints |
| Workplace safety | Federal and state workplace safety inspection databases | Entity name, address | Inspections and citations |
| Environmental | Federal and state environmental databases for the address | Address | Permits, violations, listed sites nearby |

## Log format (in legal-inventory.md)

| Date | Search | Where (site category and URL) | Terms used | Result (fact only) | Follow-up | Tracker id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Record exactly what the result showed ("2 civil cases found naming [entity], case numbers X, Y; status open"), not an interpretation ("they have legal problems").
- Note searches that found nothing as well; "no results for [terms] on [date]" is useful.
- Don't pay for searches or reports without the user's OK.
- Lien and litigation searches are repeated just before closing; add them to `small-business-closing-checklist`.
