# Master closing checklist

Filter to the deal. Give every line an owner and a due date counted back from the closing date. "T-10" means ten business days before closing. Items marked (stock) apply to a stock or membership-interest purchase; (asset) to an asset purchase; (SBA) where an SBA-guaranteed loan is involved, as the lender directs.

## Critical path (start first)
- [ ] Landlord: consent to assignment or new lease, plus estoppel if the lender asks. Owner: seller (introduces), lawyer. Start: T-30 or earlier.
- [ ] Licenses and permits: which transfer, which the buyer must apply for, how long each takes. Owner: user, lawyer. Start: T-30 or earlier.
- [ ] Lender: final approval, closing conditions list, loan documents, closing date confirmed. Owner: lender, user. T-15.
- [ ] Insurance binders effective at closing, naming the right entity and lender as required. Owner: insurance broker. T-10.
- [ ] Buying entity formed, EIN issued, bank account open with wire and ACH limits that fit closing and payroll. Owner: lawyer, CPA, user. T-15.
- [ ] Payroll provider set up for the new employer, with the first payroll date and the seller's final payroll agreed. Owner: user, CPA. T-10.
- [ ] Key contract consents (customers, suppliers, franchisor, software vendors) where required. Owner: seller, lawyer. T-20.

## Documents and signatures
- [ ] Purchase agreement (APA or SPA) final, with disclosure schedules and exhibits. Lawyers. (`small-business-purchase-agreement`)
- [ ] Bill of sale; assignment and assumption agreement (asset).
- [ ] Stock or membership interest assignment; resignations of officers; updated company records (stock).
- [ ] Seller promissory note; security agreement; lender's standby or subordination agreement (SBA).
- [ ] Non-compete and non-solicit agreement.
- [ ] Seller transition, consulting or employment agreement. (`seller-transition-plan`)
- [ ] Assignment of lease or new lease; landlord estoppel.
- [ ] Intellectual property, domain, phone number and social account transfers.
- [ ] Loan documents: note, guarantees, security agreements, lender certifications. Read before the closing day, not at it.
- [ ] Officer's or secretary's certificates, good standing certificates, resolutions authorizing the deal.
- [ ] Closing statement (settlement sheet) agreed by both sides and the lender.

## Lender conditions (copy from the term sheet or commitment letter)
- [ ] Equity injection evidenced and in the right account.
- [ ] Appraisal or business valuation, if required.
- [ ] Quality of earnings or other financial review, if required.
- [ ] Environmental report, if required.
- [ ] Life and hazard insurance assignments, if required.
- [ ] Lien searches clean or payoffs arranged.
- [ ] Any SBA forms the lender needs signed (the lender provides them; SOP rules change, so rely on the lender's current list).

## Payoffs and liens
- [ ] Payoff letters from every lender or lessor with a lien on the business's assets (asset), or with debt in the company (stock).
- [ ] UCC lien search before closing; terminations filed after payoff.
- [ ] Tax clearance or bulk-sale notices where the state uses them (ask counsel).

## Funds flow
- [ ] Sources and uses balance to the dollar (`node scripts/funds-flow.mjs`).
- [ ] Buyer's wire amount confirmed with the closing agent in writing and by phone.
- [ ] Wire instructions verified by phone on a known number. Never act on changed instructions sent by email.
- [ ] Prorations agreed: rent, utilities, prepaid contracts, deposits, accrued payroll and vacation.
- [ ] Customer deposits and prepaid work credited to the buyer where the buyer will do the work. (`working-capital-peg-for-small-business`)

## Day one (see entity-and-day-one.md)
- [ ] Bank, payroll, insurance, licenses, vendors, keys, passwords, merchant processing, phones, website, announcement.

## After closing
- [ ] Working-capital true-up: dates, who prepares, who reviews.
- [ ] Escrow or holdback release dates on the calendar.
- [ ] Seller note first payment date (and any standby period).
- [ ] Lien terminations filed; payoff confirmations received.
- [ ] Assumed name or trade name filings; sales tax and payroll tax registrations for the new entity.
- [ ] Notices to customers, suppliers and agencies as planned.
- [ ] File every signed document in the workspace (`deals/<slug>/closing/`).
