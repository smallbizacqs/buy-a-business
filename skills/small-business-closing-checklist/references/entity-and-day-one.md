# Entity questions and day-one readiness

## Entity: questions for the lawyer and CPA (don't choose for the user)

1. What entity type should hold the business (for example an LLC or a corporation), and what does each mean for liability, tax, payroll and a future sale? (Lawyer and CPA.)
2. Should there be a holding company above the operating company now, or is that for later? What does the lender allow? (Lawyer, CPA, lender.)
3. Which state should it be formed in, and must it register in other states where it operates? (Lawyer.)
4. How should the tax election be made, if any, and by when? (CPA.)
5. If there are partners or investors, what operating agreement terms are needed before closing (ownership, decisions, buy-sell, what happens if someone leaves)? (Lawyer.)
6. Does the lender need the entity formed and the account open by a set date, and which documents must name it? (Lender.)
7. Do licenses, insurance and contracts need to be in the entity's name from day one, and does any need to be in the user's own name (some professional licenses)? (Lawyer.)
8. How will the user pay themselves (salary, draws), and what payroll setup does that need? (CPA.)

## Day-one readiness

Ready means tested before closing day, not just ordered.

### Money
- [ ] Operating account in the new entity's name; online banking works; second signer if needed.
- [ ] Wire and ACH limits high enough for payroll and suppliers.
- [ ] Merchant processing (card terminals, online payments) switched or set to switch, with no gap in taking payment.
- [ ] Customer payments redirected: invoices, remittance details, autopay.
- [ ] Business credit card or purchasing method for the first weeks.
- [ ] Accounting file set up for the new entity, with opening balances from the closing statement.

### People
- [ ] Payroll provider live for the new employer; first payroll date known; the seller's final payroll agreed.
- [ ] New-hire paperwork ready for every employee (in an asset purchase, employees are usually hired by the new entity; counsel confirms).
- [ ] Benefits: what continues, what changes, and when employees are told.
- [ ] Workers' compensation coverage in force from closing.
- [ ] Announcement plan and script. (`seller-transition-plan`)

### Insurance
- [ ] General liability, property, auto, workers' compensation, professional liability as relevant, effective at closing, in the entity's name.
- [ ] Lender named as required.
- [ ] Certificates of insurance for customers or landlords who require them.

### Licenses and compliance
- [ ] Every license and permit the business operates under is transferred or reissued, or there's a written plan to operate legally until it is.
- [ ] Sales tax registration for the new entity.
- [ ] Posters and notices updated with the new employer name where required.

### Operations
- [ ] Keys, alarm codes, safe combinations.
- [ ] Passwords and admin rights for email, website, domain registrar, social accounts, software, bank portals, supplier portals, phone system. Transferred to the user, not just shared.
- [ ] Vendor accounts in the buyer's name, with credit terms where possible.
- [ ] Phone numbers and domain transferred.
- [ ] Vehicle titles and registrations.
- [ ] A copy of every signed closing document saved in the workspace.
