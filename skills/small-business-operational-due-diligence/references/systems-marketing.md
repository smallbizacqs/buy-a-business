# Systems, data and marketing

## Systems inventory

| System (accounting, payroll, job management/CRM, scheduling, phone, email, website, POS, inventory, banking, file storage) | Product category | Used for | Account holder of record | Monthly cost | Can data be exported? Format | Transfer step | Tracker id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Flag: accounts in the owner's personal name or email; systems only the owner can use; no backups; spreadsheets that hold critical data on one laptop; contracts with long terms or termination fees (counsel).

## Data quality test (sample of 20)

With read-only access, the seller present, pick 20 records at random from the job management system or CRM and check:

| Check | Count OK / 20 |
|---|---|
| Customer name, address, phone, email complete | |
| Job history matches invoices in accounting | |
| Equipment or service details recorded | |
| Next service or renewal date recorded | |
| Notes useful to someone who isn't the owner | |

Fewer than about three-quarters OK means the buyer will spend the first months rebuilding records; note it in `evaluation.md` and the transition plan. Don't copy customer personal data out of the seller's system into the workspace; record counts and examples without personal details.

## Where customers come from

Ask for the last two years by source:

| Source (referrals, repeat, search ads, organic search, review sites, social, direct mail, trade shows, builders/partners, owner's network) | Leads | Jobs won | Revenue | Spend | Cost per job won | Depends on the owner? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Questions:
1. Which sources would stop if the owner left?
2. Has any source changed recently (ad costs, a referral partner retiring, a ranking drop)?
3. Who manages the website, ads and review responses? Under whose account?
4. What's the review rating and count on the main review profiles, and the trend?
5. Is there a customer list the business markets to (email, mail), and is it allowed to be used by a new owner (counsel on privacy terms)?
6. Seasonality of leads.

Signals: growth that came from paid ads with rising costs; a single referral partner; reviews that mention the owner by name (a transition item); a website or listing the business doesn't control.

For exposure to AI substitution of the business's services, see `ai-disruption-risk-check`.

## AI tools in diligence

AI tools can help read and organize documents, compare numbers across files and draft request lists and call notes. Rules: work on the user's copies in the workspace; never upload seller documents to a service the user hasn't chosen; check every number an AI extracts against the source document before recording it; and the tracker records only checks actually done.
