# SBA facts used in the lender process (SOP 50 10 8.1)

Every line below comes from a file in `references/sources.md`. SOP 50 10 8.1 applies to loans given an SBA loan number on or after 2026-10-01 (source `sba-information-notice-5000-880695`). Before quoting a line, open its source file and check `review_by`; if the date has passed, say the rule may have changed and point to the current SOP and `sba-sop-rule-check`. Quote the proposition; don't restate it more broadly. These are rules lenders apply; none of them means a particular deal qualifies.

## What the lender looks at

| Fact | Source id |
|---|---|
| A 7(a) application to buy a business includes the purchase agreement, the business valuation, a pro forma balance sheet and three years of the seller's financial statements. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-application-business-purchase` |
| The lender analyzes the three most recent year-ends of the acquired business. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-three-years` |
| The lender compares the latest interim statement with the same period a year earlier. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-interim-statements` |
| In a change of ownership the lender must verify the seller's financial data against IRS records. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-tax-verification` |
| Lenders request tax transcripts with IRS Form 4506-C or 8821 and must follow up if the IRS hasn't responded within 10 business days. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-transcript-follow-up` |
| Owners of 20% or more and proposed guarantors give a personal financial statement (SBA Form 413 or equivalent) dated within 90 days; business financial statements must be within 120 days. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-owner-financial-statement` |
| Each co-borrower on a 7(a) loan completes and signs its own SBA Form 1919. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-form-1919` |
| The lender's credit memo describes the buyer's industry experience and who will run day-to-day operations. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-management-experience` |
| The lender must make and document a site visit to the business being acquired. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-site-visit` |
| Some types of business are ineligible for SBA loans, and the lender must check. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-ineligible-businesses` |
| A 7(a) application file includes a copy of the lease where there is one. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-application-lease-copy` |
| The lender's file must include the purchase agreement with all schedules and amendments. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-purchase-agreement-in-file` |

## Coverage and cash flow

| Fact | Source id |
|---|---|
| Coverage is tested on the last fiscal year-end or the average of the last two, at 1.25:1 for an initial acquisition or owner buyout and 1.15:1 for a business expansion. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio` |
| Historical coverage is EBITDA divided by the combined debt service after the transaction. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-definition` |
| The lender may make prudent adjustments for savings the transaction will realize. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-adjustments` |
| The lender evaluates post-closing projections but may not rely on them to meet the coverage requirement (owner-occupied special purpose property aside). | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-projections` |
| The lender runs a global cash flow analysis that must meet 1:1 coverage. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-global-cash-flow` |
| For an acquisition loan the lender analyzes whether working capital is adequate for at least the next 12 months. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-working-capital-adequacy` |

## Equity, seller notes, structure

| Fact | Source id |
|---|---|
| A buyer making an initial acquisition must inject at least 10% equity, and the lender cannot reduce or waive it. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-minimum` |
| Unborrowed cash (including a gift) and a personal loan repaid from outside the business can supply all of the required equity. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-unlimited-sources` |
| Limited sources (standby debt, standby seller debt, minority equity investments) may supply no more than half of the required equity. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-limited-sources` |
| Seller debt counts as equity only if subordinated and on full standby, with no principal or interest payments for the life of the 7(a) loan. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-debt-equity` |
| Education, advisory and agent fees do not count as equity. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-prepaid-not-equity` |
| The cost of the valuation and QoE may be passed to the borrower, and what the buyer spends on them can count toward the equity injection. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-diligence-costs` |
| In a 7(a) change of ownership, seller earnouts are prohibited; buyer rebates tied to performance are allowed. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-earnouts` |
| The seller can't stay as an officer, director, owner or employee in an initial acquisition or business expansion, but may be hired as a consultant for up to 24 months in total. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-consultant` |
| A 7(a) acquisition loan amortizes over no more than 10 years (special purpose property aside) and cannot have a balloon. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-change-of-ownership-term` |
| The largest Standard 7(a) loan is $5 million. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-max-loan` |
| In an SBA-financed asset purchase, the acquiring company is the borrower. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-asset-purchase-borrower` |
| Every individual owning 20% or more of the borrower must give an unlimited full personal guaranty. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-guaranty` |

## Valuation and QoE

| Fact | Source id |
|---|---|
| The business valuation must be ordered by and prepared for the lender; one prepared for the buyer or seller can't be used. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-for-lender` |
| It must come from an accredited, independent valuation professional with no conflict of interest. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qualified-source` |
| For a business purchase price of $350,000 or less, the lender may do its own valuation instead, unless buyer and seller are closely related. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-350k` |
| If the price is higher than the independent valuation, the difference must come from equity, not more debt. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-gap-equity` |
| For initial acquisitions and business expansions with a business purchase price of $3 million or more, the lender must obtain a QoE as well as the valuation. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-threshold` |
| A lender using delegated (PLP) authority may finish the valuation and QoE after the SBA loan number is issued, as long as they are under way and done before closing. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-plp-valuation-timing` |
| The Business Purchase Price for these rules excludes owner-occupied commercial real estate bought in the deal. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-business-purchase-price` |

## Lease, insurance, closing

| Fact | Source id |
|---|---|
| For 7(a) loans the lease term including the borrower's renewal options should equal or exceed the loan term. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lease-term` |
| The credit memo addresses life insurance (on whom and how much, or why none) and business hazard and liability insurance. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-insurance-credit-memo` |
| Before or at disbursement the lender must have its collateral and every credit-memo condition met (equity injection, standby agreements, appraisals). | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-conditions-before-disbursement` |
| A 7(a) lender may hold loan funds in escrow for no more than 5 business days to facilitate closing. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-closing-escrow` |
| For non-delegated applications sent to SBA's processing center, SBA itself decides eligibility and creditworthiness. | `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lgpc-decision` |
