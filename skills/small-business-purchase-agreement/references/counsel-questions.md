# Questions for counsel

Keep only the questions that apply; add deal-specific ones from the LOI comparison and the diligence tracker. Order the final list by money at risk. These are questions, not positions: counsel decides what to ask for and how.

## Structure and price
1. Does the agreement match the signed LOI on price, structure, payment timing, seller note, working-capital target and transition? Where it differs, does the difference favor the seller?
2. Is the buyer my new company, and am I signing anything personally (other than lender documents I've agreed to)?
3. Is everything I'm paying for listed as a purchased asset, including phone numbers, domains, websites, social accounts, software and data?
4. Which liabilities am I taking on? Is everything from before closing left with the seller, including taxes, accrued payroll and vacation, warranty work, and lawsuits?
5. How is closing working capital measured and trued up, who prepares the calculation, by when, and how are disputes resolved?
6. Has my CPA agreed the purchase price allocation, and does the agreement bind me to it?

## Protection after closing
7. Which reps should I expect for a business like this, and which are missing or limited to the seller's knowledge?
8. Have you read the disclosure schedules against the diligence record? What do they take away from my protection?
9. What are the indemnity cap, basket and survival periods, and how do they compare with what buyers usually get in deals of this size?
10. Is fraud excluded from the cap and the survival limits?
11. What secures the seller's indemnity: escrow, holdback, or set-off against the seller note? If set-off, does it work with the lender's standby or subordination terms?
12. For each diligence issue on my list, should it be a specific rep, a specific indemnity, a closing condition, an excluded liability, or a price change?

## Closing
13. What closing conditions protect me (financing, landlord consent and new lease, key consents, license transfers, no material adverse change)? If we sign and close the same day, which of them become documents I must have in hand?
14. Which ancillary documents will I sign, and which do you need to draft or review (seller note, security agreement, non-compete, transition or consulting agreement, lease assignment, IP assignments)?
15. Has the lender reviewed the draft, and are its requirements on the seller note, the seller's role after closing, the lease and insurance reflected?

## Seller's obligations
16. Is the non-compete's scope, length and geography likely to hold up in this state for this deal?
17. Does the transition or consulting agreement state the duties, hours, length and pay, and what happens if the seller stops helping?
18. What happens if the seller dies or becomes unable to help during the transition?

## Other
19. Is R&W insurance available and worth it at this deal's size, or is escrow or set-off the practical route?
20. Where would a dispute be heard, under which state's law, and is there a cheaper route (mediation, arbitration)?
21. What does your fee cover through closing, and what would be extra?
