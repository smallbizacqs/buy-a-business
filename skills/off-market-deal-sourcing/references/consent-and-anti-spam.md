# Consent, anti-spam and do-not-call: a checklist to review before sending

This is general awareness, not legal advice, and it is not complete. Rules differ by country, state and channel, and they change. The user is responsible for checking the rules that apply to them; a lawyer can confirm specifics. Don't tell the user a campaign is "compliant".

## Know which rules may apply

- **Commercial email.** Many countries regulate unsolicited commercial email. In the US, the CAN-SPAM Act is the main federal law; Canada (CASL), the UK and the EU have their own rules, several of which require prior consent for some messages. Laws commonly address honest sender information and subject lines, a working way to opt out, and honoring opt-outs promptly.
- **Calls and texts.** In the US, the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the National Do Not Call Registry rules, plus state laws, govern telemarketing calls and texts. Texting and automated or prerecorded calls, especially to mobile numbers, are generally treated more strictly than a person dialing a business line by hand. Whether a buyer's call to an owner counts as telemarketing is a question for a lawyer.
- **Privacy and data protection.** Rules about collecting and using personal data (owner names, personal emails and phones) vary by place; some apply to data about people in another country even if the user isn't there.
- **List licenses and platform terms.** Paid lists and websites have their own terms about how data may be used; professional networks restrict automated messaging.
- **Mail.** Generally the least regulated channel, but be truthful and honor requests to stop.

## Before each batch, the user confirms

- [ ] I know which country and state rules apply to me and to the recipients.
- [ ] Each message says who I am truthfully, and the subject line isn't misleading.
- [ ] Each message gives an easy way to opt out, and I have a way to record and honor opt-outs permanently.
- [ ] My emails include whatever sender details my rules require (for example a postal address).
- [ ] I'm not texting or auto-dialing anyone without checking the consent rules for that.
- [ ] I've removed anyone marked `do_not_contact` or `opted-out` in `targets.csv`.
- [ ] I've checked the list vendor's or website's terms allow this use.
- [ ] I'm sending at a pace and from an account I control; no purchased "blast" tools without understanding their rules and mine.

## Agent behavior

- Show this checklist before the user's first send in each channel, and again if they change channel or region.
- Never send, schedule or queue messages for the user. Save drafts only.
- If the user asks the agent to send in bulk, decline and offer drafts plus this checklist.
