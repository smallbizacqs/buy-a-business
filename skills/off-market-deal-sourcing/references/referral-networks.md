# Referral networks

Owners often talk to their accountant, lawyer or banker before anyone else about selling. A buyer who is known, trusted and specific to these people hears about some opportunities early.

## Who to build relationships with

| Role | Why they hear about sales | What to offer them |
|---|---|---|
| CPAs and bookkeepers serving small businesses | They see owners' finances and retirement plans first | A clear buy box; a buyer who treats their client well; respect for confidentiality |
| Estate-planning and business attorneys | Succession and estate plans raise the question of selling | Same |
| Community bank lenders and SBA lenders | Existing borrowers retiring; other buyers' failed deals | A buyer with a financing profile they understand |
| Wealth managers and financial planners | Owners' retirement planning | Same |
| Commercial insurance agents | Know every business in a trade locally | A future client |
| Industry suppliers, distributors and equipment dealers | Hear owners talk about retiring | A future customer who keeps buying |
| Trade association staff and peer-group leaders | Know who's winding down | Membership, participation |
| Brokers who handle smaller or off-list deals | Pre-market conversations | Credibility (`business-broker-relations`) |

## How to approach

1. Ask for advice, not deals: "I'm searching for a [industry] business in [area]; what should I know?"
2. Leave a one-page buy box summary (from `buy-box.yaml`): who you are, industries, size, area, how you'd treat employees and the owner's legacy, financing path, how to reach you.
3. Follow up every few months with a short, real update (what you've learned, criteria changes). Don't pester.
4. Never ask them to breach a client's confidence. They will introduce an owner only with the owner's OK.
5. If a referral-fee question comes up, the user should ask a lawyer: some professionals face rules on referral payments, and it must be disclosed to the parties.

## Introduction note (draft for the user to send)

> Subject: Introduction: local buyer for a [industry] business
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> [Mutual contact / "I found your firm through the [association] directory"]. I'm [Name], and I'm looking to buy and run one [industry] business in [area]. [One line background.]
>
> I'm not asking for any client information. I'd value 20 minutes of your perspective on the local market, and I've attached a one-page summary of what I'm looking for in case it's ever useful to an owner you work with.
>
> [Name] · [phone]
