# Interview questions by role

Ask each candidate the same questions so answers compare. Record answers in `notes.md`; mark quotes as quotes.

## Lawyer

1. How many small-business purchases (under about $5M) did you handle in the last two years, and on which side?
2. Who will do the work day to day, and who reviews it?
3. Will you quote a fixed fee for (a) LOI review, (b) legal diligence, (c) purchase agreement and closing documents? What's excluded?
4. How do you use templates or AI tools to keep cost down, and how do you check the output?
5. How do you work with an SBA or bank lender's closing requirements and the lender's counsel?
6. What's your typical turnaround on a first draft or a markup?
7. How do you decide what to fight for and what to let go in a small deal?
8. Do you have any relationship with the seller, the broker or the seller's lawyer?
9. What happens to the fee if the deal dies halfway?
10. Can I speak to two buyers you represented?

## CPA

1. Have you advised buyers on acquisitions? How many in the last two years, what size?
2. Will you review the seller's tax returns and financials and tell me where they don't tie?
3. How would you think about asset vs stock for this seller's entity type, and when should we talk about allocation?
4. Can you help set up the new entity's books, payroll and tax accounts for closing day?
5. Do you offer a lighter financial review, and how is it different from a QoE?
6. Fixed fee or hourly with a cap for the deal work? What's excluded?
7. Who will do the work?
8. Will you be my ongoing CPA after closing, and what does that cost?

## QoE provider

1. How many QoE engagements on deals of this size in the last year? In this industry?
2. What's in your standard scope: proof of cash, SDE/EBITDA adjustments, revenue analysis, customer concentration, working capital, debt-like items? What's extra?
3. Fixed fee for that scope? Timeline from receiving documents?
4. What do you need from the seller, and in what form? (Ask for their request list.)
5. Will the lender accept your report? Have you worked with lenders on SBA deals?
6. Who does the work, and who signs off?
7. Will you tell me early if you find something that might kill the deal, before the full report?
8. Can the scope be phased (proof of cash first, then the rest)?

## Lender

1. How many business acquisition loans did you close last year? In this size range and industry?
2. What would you need to see before issuing a term sheet? (Get their checklist.)
3. What equity injection, seller note terms and coverage do you require for this deal? (For SBA 7(a) initial acquisitions, SOP 50 10 8.1 sets a 10% equity minimum the lender can't waive, source `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-minimum`, and 1.25:1 coverage, source `sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio`; the lender's credit policy may be stricter. Check the sources' `review_by` dates.)
4. Do you require a QoE, a business valuation, life insurance, real estate collateral? Who orders and who pays?
5. What's your timeline from full package to approval to closing? Who makes the credit decision?
6. What fees will I pay (packaging, guarantee fee, closing costs, prepayment)? Who pays a loan broker, if any?
7. What usually causes delays or declines on deals like mine?

## Insurance broker

1. Which carriers do you place for businesses in this industry?
2. Can you review the seller's current policies and five years of loss runs and tell me what's missing?
3. Are any of the seller's policies claims-made, needing tail coverage?
4. What will the lender require (property, liability, life, disability), and can you bind by the closing date?
5. What's your timeline for quotes once I send the information?
