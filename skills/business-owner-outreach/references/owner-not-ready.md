# When an owner isn't ready to sell yet

"Not now" is not "no". Many owners who sell eventually were not ready at the first contact. Sourcing
works as many long conversations, so a light, respectful follow-up plan matters more than a hard ask.

## First, learn what "not ready" means

Ask, then record the answer in `notes.md`:

- "Is it timing, or is it that you'd never sell?"
- "What would need to be true for it to make sense?" (a number, a successor for staff, finishing a
  project, a family decision, a tax year)
- "Roughly when might you revisit it: next year, a few years, longer?"
- "Would it be all right if I checked in now and then?" (If they say no, mark the row `passed` with
  "owner asked not to be contacted" and stop.)

## Default cadence (adjust to what the owner asked for)

| When | Touch | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Within 2 days | Thank-you note | Close the loop on the conversation. |
| 1 month | Short check-in or something useful (an article on their industry, a local event) | Stay remembered without asking. |
| 3 months | Brief call or note | Ask how the season or year is going. |
| Every 3 to 6 months after | Note or call, alternating | Keep the thread alive; ask about the "what would need to be true" item. |
| On a trigger | Call | Owner mentioned an event (end of tax year, a lease renewal, a key employee retiring, a birthday milestone). |

Put the next date in `pipeline.csv` `next_date` and a one-line reason in `next_step`, so the user's
search plan (`acquisition-search-plan`) can surface it.

## Rules

- Each touch has a reason other than "are you ready yet?".
- Keep messages short (three to five sentences).
- Never pressure with deadlines the user doesn't really have.
- Honor any request to stop, immediately, and record it.
- Check the anti-spam and calling rules that apply to the user's channel and location before any
  repeated email or call program (see `off-market-deal-sourcing`).

## Draft check-in notes (for the user to edit and send)

**One month:**

> Jane, I hope the busy season has been good to you. I saw the new county contract for road work
> near you and thought of the crew. No agenda; just wanted to say thanks again for the time in
> November. Sam

**Six months:**

> Jane, it's been about six months since we met at the shop. When we talked, you mentioned you'd
> want to see Mike settled as foreman before thinking about anything else. How's that going? I'm
> still very interested whenever the timing is right, and happy to buy you lunch again either way.
> Sam

**Owner signals readiness:**

> Jane, thanks for calling. I'd be glad to pick up where we left off. Would next Tuesday or Wednesday
> work to sit down? If it's helpful, I can sign an NDA before you share any numbers.
> Sam
