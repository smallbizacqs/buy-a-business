# Value drivers: what moves the multiple for this business

Score each driver as **raises**, **neutral** or **lowers** the multiple relative to the comparables, and cite the evidence (file, page, or "seller's statement, unverified"). Drivers with no evidence are "unknown", not neutral. Use the result to place the business low, middle or high in the comparable range; don't add or subtract fixed amounts per driver.

| Driver | Raises | Lowers | Evidence to look at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earnings trend | Steady or rising over 3+ years, TTM confirms | Falling, or one good year | Returns, monthly P&L, TTM |
| Earnings quality | Add-backs few and supported; books tie to bank and returns | Large unsupported add-backs; proof-of-cash gaps | Earnings section; verification.md |
| Recurring revenue | Contracts, memberships, maintenance agreements, repeat customers | Project-by-project, re-won each time | Contracts, revenue by customer by year |
| Customer concentration | No customer above a small share of revenue | One or a few customers carry the business | Revenue by customer (risks section) |
| Owner dependence | Owner works on the business; managers run daily work; customers know the team | Owner sells, estimates, holds the license and the relationships | Org chart, owner's week, who customers call |
| Team | Tenured staff, documented roles, a second-in-command | Key person risk, high turnover, hiring hard | Payroll history, tenure, interviews (later) |
| Systems | Software, documented processes, clean books | Knowledge in the owner's head | Walkthrough, process documents |
| Size | Larger earnings than the comparables | Smaller | Comparables table |
| Industry and demand | Essential, non-discretionary service; regulatory or licensing barriers to entry | Discretionary, fad-driven, or exposed to AI or substitution | Industry data with a source; `ai-disruption-risk-check` |
| Capital needs | Light; equipment in good condition | Heavy; deferred replacement | Asset list, ages, capital spending history |
| Margins | At or above peers the user can document | Below, without a fixable reason | P&L vs any sourced benchmark |
| Growth levers | Clear, cheap, documented (pricing, an untapped channel) | None obvious | Buyer's plan, labeled as the buyer's |
| Transferability | Leases assignable, licenses transferable, contracts assignable | Lease ending, license personal to owner, change-of-control clauses | Lease, licenses, contracts (lawyer reviews) |

## Rules

- A buyer's growth plan raises what the business is worth **to the buyer**, not what the buyer should pay. Paying for the buyer's own future work is a choice to make deliberately, and lenders won't count projected growth toward coverage.
- If several "lowers" drivers stack (owner dependence plus concentration plus decline), the right answer may be below the comparable range or a pass, not the bottom of the range.
- Write the placement as one sentence: "Low end of the range because owner holds every customer relationship and SDE fell two years running (P&L 2024–2025)."
