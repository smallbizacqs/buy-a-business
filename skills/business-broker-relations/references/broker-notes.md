# Draft notes to brokers

All drafts are saved under `## Drafts (not sent)` in `deals/<slug>/notes.md`. The user edits and
sends them. Fill bracketed parts from the workspace; never invent experience or funds.

## First note about a listing

Structure: the listing, why it fits the user (a real connection), who the user is in one line, the
financing in one line, the ask.

> Subject: [Listing title / listing number]: inquiry from Sam Lee
>
> Hi [broker name],
>
> I'm interested in the [industry] business in [area] you have listed ([listing number]). I spent
> ten years running field operations for a distribution company, and a route-based service
> business with a steady commercial customer base is exactly what I'm looking for.
>
> I'd run the business myself. I'm buying with my own equity and an SBA 7(a) loan, and I'm open to
> seller financing. Happy to provide proof of funds once we're under NDA.
>
> Could you send the NDA and, once signed, the CIM? I can turn the NDA around within a day. I've
> attached a one-page summary of what I'm looking for.
>
> Thanks,
> Sam Lee · (555) 010-0000

What to change per listing: the connection sentence. A specific, true reason this business fits the
user is the single strongest line.

## After reading the CIM

> Hi [broker name],
>
> Thank you for the CIM on [business]. I've read it and I'm still interested. A few questions before
> I can give you a view on value:
>
> 1. [Specific question, e.g. "The top customer is 22% of revenue; how long has that relationship
>    lasted and is there a contract?"]
> 2. [e.g. "What does the owner do day to day, and who handles estimating?"]
> 3. [e.g. "Are the add-backs supported by documents I could see after an initial indication?"]
>
> Could we set up a call this week? Tuesday after 2pm or Wednesday morning both work.
>
> Sam

## Asking for financials before an offer

> Hi [broker name], to put together a serious indication of interest I'd need [the last three years
> of tax returns, the year-to-date P&L and balance sheet, and the add-back schedule]. Is the owner
> comfortable sharing those now, or would they prefer an initial indication first?

## Passing

> Hi [broker name], thank you for sending [business]. I'm going to pass: [one honest reason, e.g.
> "the revenue concentration is more than I'm comfortable with" or "it's outside my price range"].
> I'd be glad to see anything else that fits my criteria (attached). Thanks again.

## Following up after silence

> Hi [broker name], following up on my note from [date] about [listing]. I'm still interested and
> can sign an NDA today. Is the business still available?
