# NDA checklist: points to raise with a lawyer

A seller's NDA (non-disclosure or confidentiality agreement) is usually the first document a buyer
signs. Most are short and standard-looking, but some contain terms that matter later. This checklist
helps the user notice terms and turn them into questions for their lawyer. It does not say what any
clause means or whether to sign.

Write the result in `deals/<slug>/notes.md` under `## NDA points for my lawyer`, quoting the clause
number and its words, then the question.

## What to look for and the question to ask

| Look for | Question for the lawyer |
|---|---|
| **Parties.** Who signs: the user personally, or a company? Is the seller, the broker, or both a party? | Should I sign personally or through an entity, and does that change anything? |
| **What counts as confidential.** Everything shared, or only items marked confidential? Does it include the fact that the business is for sale? | Is the definition reasonable, and what is excluded (public information, things I already knew)? |
| **Term.** How long the obligations last (some have none stated). | Is the length reasonable for a deal this size? |
| **Who I may share with.** Advisors, lenders, investors, spouse? | Can I share with my lender, CPA, lawyer, quality-of-earnings provider and investors, and do they need to sign too? |
| **No-contact terms.** Bans on contacting employees, customers, suppliers, landlord, or the owner directly. | What contact is forbidden, for how long, and what happens if they contact me? |
| **Non-solicit / no-hire.** Bans on hiring the seller's employees. | How long does it last, and does it apply if I don't buy the business? |
| **Non-circumvention.** Bans on dealing with the seller outside the broker. | What does this stop me doing, and for how long? |
| **Fees.** Any fee owed by the buyer to the broker, or any promise to pay a fee if the deal closes. | Am I agreeing to pay anyone anything? |
| **Return or destruction.** Must I return or destroy materials, including notes and analysis? | Can I keep copies needed for my records or my lender? |
| **Remedies.** Injunctions, attorney fees, damages, indemnities. | Is anything here unusual or one-sided? |
| **Governing law and venue.** Which state's law; where disputes are heard. | Is this a problem for me? |
| **No obligation.** Does it say neither side must do a deal? | Does anything here commit me to more than confidentiality? |
| **Mutual or one-way.** Does it protect the user's information too? | Should it be mutual? |
| **Residuals or "no reliance" language.** Statements that the seller makes no promise about accuracy. | What does this mean for relying on the CIM later? |

## Practical notes (not legal interpretation)

- Read it fully before signing, even if the broker says it is standard.
- Keep a signed copy in `deals/<slug>/`.
- Track who the user has shared materials with (advisors, lender), since many NDAs limit this.
- Do not contact employees, customers or suppliers until the user's lawyer confirms the NDA allows it
  and the seller agrees.

## Lawyer or template?

Some buyers sign small-deal NDAs after reading them carefully, or use templates (for example,
openagreements.org has NDA templates). Buyers who hire counsel can look for this category:
AI-using law firms with fixed fees that are lean and run by an attorney with big-law experience.
