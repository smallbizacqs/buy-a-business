# Early red flags

Rate each flag found: **pass now** (a deal-breaker for this user), **ask before an offer** (could be
fine with a good answer), or **check in diligence** (needs documents). The user's `buy-box.yaml`
deal-breakers override this list: a flag the user named as a deal-breaker is "pass now".

Cite evidence for every flag: the page of the CIM, the line of the teaser, or the user's notes of
what the broker said. No evidence, no flag; write it as an open question instead.

## Earnings and numbers

| Flag | Default rating |
|---|---|
| Seller won't share tax returns before an offer, or numbers aren't from tax returns and no reason is given | ask before an offer |
| Add-backs are a large part of stated earnings, or include ordinary running costs (rent, wages for needed staff, repairs) | ask before an offer |
| Revenue or earnings falling for two or more years without a clear one-time reason | ask before an offer |
| A single unusually strong recent year carrying the price | ask before an offer |
| "Cash income" or "unreported revenue" offered as part of earnings | pass now, or remove it from the numbers (lenders and buyers generally can't rely on it) |
| Asking price that only works on projections | ask before an offer |
| Numbers that don't add up within the CIM (totals, margins, years) | ask before an offer |

## Customers and market

| Flag | Default rating |
|---|---|
| One customer is a large share of revenue, or a few customers are most of it | ask before an offer |
| Revenue depends on a contract that ends soon, or on a relationship personal to the owner | ask before an offer |
| Business depends on one supplier, platform or referral source | ask before an offer |
| Industry facing a regulatory change, reimbursement cut, or technology shift | ask before an offer |

## Owner and people

| Flag | Default rating |
|---|---|
| Owner is the main salesperson, estimator, licensed professional or face of the business, with no plan to transfer that | ask before an offer |
| Owner offers little or no transition time | ask before an offer |
| Key employees likely to leave or not yet told; a key employee is a family member of the owner | check in diligence |
| A required license is held by the owner personally and the user can't obtain it | pass now unless solved |

## Assets, legal and place

| Flag | Default rating |
|---|---|
| Lease ends soon, has no renewal, or the landlord's consent is uncertain | ask before an offer |
| Old fleet or equipment with no capital spending history | ask before an offer |
| Pending lawsuits, regulatory actions, liens or tax problems mentioned or hinted | ask before an offer |
| Environmental exposure (fuel, chemicals, older industrial sites) | check in diligence |

## Process and behavior

| Flag | Default rating |
|---|---|
| Pressure to sign or offer fast without information | ask before an offer |
| Story changes between conversations (reason for sale, numbers, owner hours) | ask before an offer |
| Broker or seller refuses basic questions after an NDA | ask before an offer |
| Business has been listed for a long time with price cuts and no clear reason | ask before an offer |

## How to write it up

```markdown
## Red flags
| Flag | Evidence | Rating | Question to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top customer 38% of revenue | CIM p. 12 | ask before an offer | Contract term? How long has the relationship lasted? Who manages it? |
```

For a deeper, scored risk picture after documents arrive, hand off to `small-business-risk-assessment`.
