**Short answer.** Expect to send the lender financial statements on a schedule, keep the required insurance in force, meet any financial tests in the loan agreement, and respect limits on things like distributions, owner pay and payments to the seller. The details come from your own loan documents, which vary by lender, so read them before closing. Breaking a covenant can be a default even if every payment is on time. What happens next depends on the documents and the lender, and can range from a written waiver or added conditions to, in serious cases, a demand for the full balance.

### Where the obligations come from

Your note and loan agreement set the rules. SBA does not require a separate loan agreement; a lender that uses one on its other loans must use one on SBA loans, and may use its own form.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-loan-agreement] Lenders making 7(a) Small and SBA Express loans may largely use their own notes and servicing documents.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-small-express-lender-documents] Once an SBA 7(a) loan is disbursed, the lender's servicing follows a separate SBA rulebook, SOP 50 57.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-servicing-liquidation]

### Reporting

- **Financial statements.** If your loan documents require periodic financial statements, SBA's servicing rules say the lender must request and review them in a timely way.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-financial-statements] Lenders are told not to waive or loosen these requirements (for example, from reviewed to compiled statements, or less often) without a prudent reason.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-financial-statement-waiver]
- **Late statements cost you.** A lender may charge a fee for past due statements and may refuse to consider your requests, such as a deferment or a collateral release, until you catch up.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-past-due-financials]

Put the reporting calendar in place from the first month. See [financial controls and KPIs](/q/financial-controls-kpis).

### Covenants

A covenant is a promise in the loan documents to do, or not do, something while the loan is outstanding.

- **Financial covenants.** In an acquisition the lender may put a debt service coverage covenant in the loan agreement.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-covenants] Find out exactly how the ratio is calculated, which statements it uses, and how often it's tested.
- **Management covenants.** Loan documents can limit owner compensation, fixed asset purchases, working capital levels and dividends, and lenders are told not to modify these without a prudent basis.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-management-covenants]
- **Investor distributions.** If investors' money counted toward your SBA equity injection, they can receive only tax distributions until the 7(a) loan is paid off.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-investor-distributions]
- **Seller standby.** A lender should not loosen a standby agreement to allow payments to the seller without a prudent basis, and a standby that counted as equity can change only if the SOP in effect at approval allows it.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-standby-modification] See [seller payments after closing](/q/seller-payments-after-closing).
- **Insurance.** The lender must check that required insurance stays in force for the life of the loan.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-insurance-monitoring] If required hazard coverage lapses, the lender may buy it itself and recover the cost from you.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-force-placed-insurance] See [insurance after closing](/q/post-closing-insurance).

### If you miss a covenant or a payment

On an SBA 7(a) loan, a default is any event listed in the note or other loan documents, not only a missed payment.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-default-definition] SBA Express loans may include non-financial default provisions, which put the loan in default even when every payment has been made.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-express-non-financial-default] A lender cannot accelerate the loan (demand the whole balance) or liquidate collateral unless there has been a default under the note.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-acceleration]

If the problem is a temporary cash shortfall, a lender may defer payments for a stated period, generally with interest still accruing.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-deferment] Behind all of this sits your personal guaranty, which is unlimited for every 20% owner.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-guaranty]

In practice:

- Track each covenant monthly, and if a miss is coming, call the lender before the deadline with the numbers and your plan.
- Get any waiver or amendment in writing. Don't treat silence as a waiver.
- Have your lawyer read the default and cure provisions before you sign, not after a miss.

### Questions to ask before closing

- What financial statements, tax returns and personal financial statements do you want, how often, and by when?
- Which covenants apply, how is each calculated, and when is it first tested?
- Is there a cure period or a right to fix a miss?
- What limits apply to owner pay, distributions, new debt, capital spending and payments to the seller?
