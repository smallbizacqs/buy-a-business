**Short answer.** A personal guarantee (in SBA documents, a "guaranty") is your promise to repay the business's loan yourself if the business can't. With an unlimited guaranty, your personal assets beyond what you put into the deal are exposed. For an SBA 7(a) loan, under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), every individual who owns 20% or more of the borrower must give an unlimited full guaranty,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-guaranty] so there's little room to limit it for a buyer who owns the business.

**Who must guarantee an SBA loan**

- Every individual owning 20% or more, directly or indirectly: unlimited and full.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-guaranty]
- A spouse owning less than 20%, when spouses and minor children together own 20% or more: in full.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-spouse-guaranty]
- Others the lender requires, full or limited, regardless of ownership (for example, someone critical to operations).[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-other-guarantors]

**What it can reach**

If the business defaults and the collateral doesn't cover the loan, the lender can pursue the guarantors personally. Separately, when the business assets don't fully secure the loan, the lender must take available equity in personal real estate owned by 20% owners and guarantors.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-real-estate] It need not take real estate with less than 25% equity,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-real-estate-equity-threshold] and a lien can be limited to the shortfall or to 150% of the equity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lien-limit] See [collateral and your house](/q/collateral-home).

**Ways buyers manage the risk**

- Size the deal so the coverage cushion is real, not just at the minimum. See [stress-testing](/q/stress-test).
- Understand which assets are exposed and how state law treats them; that's a question for a lawyer in your state. See [asset protection](/q/asset-protection).
- Ask about guarantee insurance; see [insuring a guarantee](/q/pg-insurance).
- Outside SBA lending, guarantee terms are part of the loan negotiation with each lender.

Talk it through with your spouse or family before signing; see [talking through the risk](/q/spouse-family).
