**Short answer.** Cost depends on the size of the business, how clean the books are, and the scope you ask for, so get written quotes from several providers for the same scope. A lighter review, such as a proof of cash alone, can be enough when the SBA doesn't require a QoE and the books are simple. When the SBA does require one, the scope is set by the SBA rules, not by you. An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

**What an SBA-required QoE must include.** Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026):

- A **cash proof**, reconciling bank statements to the income statement and tax returns, for the trailing 12 months and the last two fiscal years.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-cash-proof]
- Every **add-back and adjustment** to reported earnings documented, and an assessment of **revenue quality**, including customer concentration and contract continuity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-revenue-quality]
- **Independence**: done for the lender, and not prepared by or for the seller.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-not-for-seller]

**Lighter options buyers use when it's their choice:**

- **Proof of cash only**: matches deposits to reported revenue. It catches the most basic problems.
- **Focused review**: proof of cash plus a check of the largest add-backs and the top customers.
- **Full QoE**: the above plus working capital, revenue trends and a normalized earnings figure.

**Questions to ask a provider:** What exactly is in scope and what isn't? Who is the report addressed to, and can the lender rely on it? How long will it take once you have the documents? Is the fee fixed? What documents do you need from the seller?

**Paying for it.** With an SBA loan, the cost of financial diligence reports may be passed to the borrower, and what you spend can count toward your equity injection.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-diligence-costs] See [do I need a QoE?](/q/qoe-need) and [staging diligence spending](/q/phased-diligence).
