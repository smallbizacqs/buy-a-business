**Short answer.** The lender, not the SBA, does the analysis and makes the loan; the SBA guarantees part of it.[^sba-7a-loans-page] For a business purchase, the lender checks the seller's historical numbers against IRS records, orders an independent valuation, tests whether historical cash flow covers all the new debt, and looks at whether you can run the business. The rules below are from SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026).

**The business's numbers**

- The three most recent year-end financial statements, at the highest level of reporting available.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-three-years]
- The latest interim statement compared with the same period a year earlier.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-interim-statements]
- The seller's financial data verified against IRS records.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-tax-verification]

**The price**

- A business valuation ordered by and prepared for the lender; one prepared for the buyer or seller can't be used.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-for-lender] The lender checks the valuation's numbers against the seller's IRS transcripts.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-verified]
- A quality of earnings (QoE) report as well, for an initial acquisition or business expansion priced at $3 million or more.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-threshold]

**Repayment**

- Historical debt service coverage of at least 1.25:1 for an initial acquisition.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio] Projections are reviewed but can't be relied on to meet the ratio (special purpose property aside).[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-projections] See [DSCR](/q/dscr).
- Your pay from the business must cover your personal obligations and living expenses.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-owner-compensation]

**You and the business**

- Your industry experience and who will manage day-to-day operations.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-management-experience]
- A documented site visit to the business being acquired.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-site-visit]
- A certification that you can't get the credit on reasonable terms without SBA help.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-credit-elsewhere]

These are the SBA's requirements; lenders differ in how they judge the rest of the credit, so two lenders can reach different answers on the same deal.

## What practitioners say
- Helen Guo works an SBA 7(a) example: $100K a year in loan payments needs $125K of cash flow at 1.25x coverage, measured on historical results, not a forecast.[^c237676]
