**Short answer.** A quality of earnings (QoE) report is an independent accountant's check of whether the seller's reported earnings are real and likely to continue. If you're buying with an SBA 7(a) loan (a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration) and the business purchase price is $3 million or more, the lender must get one for an initial acquisition or business expansion. Below that it's your choice, and it's worth more the less you trust the books, the bigger the add-backs, and the more of your own money is at stake.

**When SBA requires one.** Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), for initial acquisitions and business expansions with a business purchase price of $3 million or more, the lender must get a QoE in addition to the business valuation. The threshold is measured before buyer equity, seller debt or other financing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-threshold] Owner buyouts and ESOP (employee stock ownership plan) or cooperative deals are exempt.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-owner-buyout-exempt] See [the SBA's $3 million rule](/q/sba-qoe-3m-rule).

**When buyers often get one anyway:**

- The books are kept on a cash basis, or by the owner without an outside accountant.
- Earnings depend on large add-backs (owner expenses added back to profit).
- A lot of revenue is in cash, or the business has many related-party dealings.
- Tax returns, financial statements and bank deposits don't obviously agree.
- The price is a big share of your net worth.

**If you commission your own.** A lender may have a QoE you commissioned reviewed by one of its approved vendors, instead of ordering a new one.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-buyer-commissioned] Ask your lender before you hire anyone, so the work can be used. The cost of financial diligence reports may be passed on to the borrower, and what you spend on them can count toward your equity injection.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-diligence-costs]

**What a QoE doesn't do:** it isn't an audit, and it doesn't value the business or tell you whether to buy. See [QoE cost and scope](/q/qoe-cost-scope).
