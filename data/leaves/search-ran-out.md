**Short answer.** You have three broad choices: extend the search with new money or a new plan, change course to a different way of getting into ownership, or stop. Which is right depends on what your funnel shows, how much runway you have, what you've promised any investors, and what you and your family want. Decide at a point you set in advance, not when the bank balance forces it.

**First, read what happened**

Look at your funnel before deciding anything. See [deal funnel math](/q/deal-funnel-math).

- **Too few conversations?** The problem is sourcing volume or channels.
- **Conversations but no financials?** Trust or credibility with owners. See [earning an owner's trust](/q/earn-trust).
- **Financials but no deals worth offering on?** The buy box may not match what's for sale at your price.
- **LOIs (letters of intent) that didn't get signed?** Price, terms or competition. See [winning without the highest offer](/q/buyer-of-choice).
- **Signed deals that didn't close?** Diligence, financing, or deal selection.

The answer tells you whether more time would help or whether something has to change.

**Option 1: Extend**

- **Self-funded searchers:** decide how much more of your savings you'll spend, and protect the cash you need for the down payment. See [paying living costs during the search](/q/search-budget).
- **Funded searchers (traditional search funds):** an extension may need your investors' agreement and more capital; your agreements set the terms. Bring them the funnel data and a specific plan. See [traditional search funds](/q/traditional-search-fund).
- **Change the plan, not just the deadline.** Widen the buy box (size, industry, geography), add channels, or work a deal pipeline you've neglected.

**Option 2: Change course**

- **Search part time** while working. See [keeping your day job](/q/keep-day-job).
- **Switch models,** for example from a funded search to a self-funded search or an independent sponsor approach (finding a deal first, then raising money for it). See [self-funded search](/q/self-funded-search) and [independent sponsors](/q/independent-sponsor).
- **Partner** with another buyer who has a deal or capital. See [partner or solo](/q/partner-or-solo).
- **Buy smaller** than you planned, if the economics still work.
- **Work in a business first,** as a manager or general manager with a path to ownership. See [working in a business before buying](/q/work-in-business-first).

**Option 3: Stop**

Stopping is a legitimate outcome. What you learned about evaluating businesses, financing and negotiation carries into a job, an operating role, investing, or a later search. If you raised money, close out properly: report to your investors, settle any obligations under your agreements, and wind down the search entity if you formed one, with your lawyer and CPA.

**Questions to settle before you decide**

1. What exactly do my agreements with investors, a spouse or a partner say about timing and money?
2. How many months of living costs can I spend without touching the equity I'd need for a deal?
3. Is there a live deal close enough to finish? A deal under LOI can be worth extending for; a vague pipeline is a weaker reason.
4. What would I need to see in the next three months to keep going?
5. What does my household want?

**Set the decision point early**

The easiest version of this decision is the one you make at the start: a date or a remaining-cash level at which you'll review, with the questions above. See [how long a search takes](/q/search-timeline).
