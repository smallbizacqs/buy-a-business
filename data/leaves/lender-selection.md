**Short answer.** Pick a lender that closes SBA acquisition loans regularly, has delegated (PLP) authority so it can approve without waiting on SBA, and answers your questions clearly and quickly. Get term sheets from two or three, compare them line by line, and judge how each handled your deal's weak spots, not only the rate. SBA guarantees 7(a) loans, but the lender makes the loan and decides.[^sba-7a-loans-page]

### Categories of SBA lenders

- **Banks with SBA departments**, from community banks to national banks. Their appetite for acquisitions, industries and loan sizes varies widely.
- **Non-bank SBA lenders** licensed to make 7(a) loans. Some focus on acquisitions.
- **Certified Development Companies**, which make 504 loans with a partner bank for real estate and equipment.[^sba-504-page-cdcs] See [7(a) or 504](/q/7a-vs-504).
- **Loan brokers and packagers**, who don't lend but shop your deal. SBA doesn't require you to use one, and lenders and agents must tell you so.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-agent-not-required] If you pay one, the fee is disclosed on SBA Form 159, signed by you, the agent and the lender.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-form-159]

### Delegated authority matters for speed

A lender with Preferred Lender Program (PLP) authority approves the loan itself; SBA doesn't review its credit analysis before issuing a loan number.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-plp-review] Without it, SBA's processing center decides eligibility and creditworthiness and approves or declines the loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lgpc-decision] A PLP lender may also finish the business valuation and QoE after the loan number is issued, as long as they're under way and done before closing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-plp-valuation-timing] Ask whether the lender will process your loan under PLP authority.

### Questions to ask

1. How many SBA acquisition loans did you close in the last year, and in what industries and sizes?
2. Will this loan be processed under your PLP authority?
3. Who underwrites it, and who will I talk to week to week?
4. What's your timeline from complete package to approval, and to closing?
5. What do you need from me, and when, for the valuation and any QoE?
6. How do you treat a seller note, a working capital line, and real estate in the deal?
7. What minimum liquidity do you want me to keep after closing?
8. What would make you decline this deal?

### Comparing term sheets

- **Rate.** The spread over prime and how often it resets. SBA caps the variable spread by loan size,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-max-variable-rates] but lenders can price below the cap.
- **Fees.** Lenders can't charge origination, broker or referral fees, points or their own prepayment penalties.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-prohibited-fees] They may charge reasonable packaging fees[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-packaging-fees] and must itemize fees in writing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-fee-itemization] Lender legal fees passed to you must be billed hourly.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lender-legal-hourly]
- **Structure.** Equity required, how the seller note is treated, collateral demands (including your home), and any conditions that could change before closing.
- **Who's committed.** A term sheet isn't an approval. Ask what's left to decide and by whom.

### Red flags

- Promises of approval before the lender has seen tax returns.
- Pressure to sign an exclusive or pay a large deposit early.
- Vague answers on PLP status or who makes the decision.
- Fees that aren't itemized.
