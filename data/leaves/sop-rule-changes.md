**Short answer.** SOP 50 10 is the SBA rulebook lenders follow for 7(a) and 504 loans, and it has changed four times since mid-2023: version 7 (effective August 1, 2023), 7.1 (November 15, 2023), 8 (June 1, 2025) and 8.1 (October 1, 2026).[^sba-sop-50-10-versions] SOP 50 10 8.1 applies to applications that get an SBA loan number on or after October 1, 2026.[^sba-information-notice-5000-880695] For buyers, the biggest swings have been in how seller notes count as equity and who may own the business.

### Why the version matters

Lenders underwrite to the version in force when your loan is numbered. Advice, videos and lender checklists from even a year ago may describe rules that no longer apply. When you hear an SBA "rule," ask which SOP version it comes from.

### Seller notes as equity

| Version | Rule |
|---|---|
| 7.1 | A seller note could count as equity if on full standby for the first 24 months, or on partial standby (interest only) when historical cash flow covered the payments and at least a quarter of the injection came from elsewhere.[^sba-sop-50-10-7-1-seller-standby] |
| 8 | Only on full standby for the life of the SBA loan, and for no more than half of the required injection.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-seller-standby] |
| 8.1 | Only if subordinated and on full standby, with no principal or interest for the term of the 7(a) loan,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-debt-equity] and, with the other "limited" sources, for no more than half of the required injection.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-limited-sources] |

See [seller note standby](/q/seller-note-standby) and [seller note as equity](/q/seller-note-as-equity).

### Who may own the business

| Version | Rule |
|---|---|
| 7.1 | Businesses at least 51% owned and controlled by lawful permanent residents could be eligible.[^sba-sop-50-10-7-1-lpr-ownership] |
| 8 | 100% of owners and required guarantors had to be U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals or lawful permanent residents.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-citizenship] |
| 8.1 | 100% must be U.S. citizens or U.S. nationals living in the U.S.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-citizenship-owners] Lawful permanent residents are Ineligible Persons.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lpr-ineligible] |

See [the citizenship rule](/q/citizenship-rule).

### Other SOP 50 10 8.1 rules buyers run into

- **Equity.** An Initial Acquisition needs at least 10% equity, and the lender can't reduce or waive it.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-minimum] See [equity injection](/q/equity-injection).
- **Quality of earnings.** For an Initial Acquisition or Business Expansion with a business purchase price of $3 million or more, the lender must get a quality of earnings (QoE) report as well as the valuation.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-threshold] See [the $3 million QoE rule](/q/sba-qoe-3m-rule).
- **Partial buyouts by newcomers.** In an owner buyout, someone who hasn't worked in the business for 24 months may buy less than 50% and can't become the largest shareholder.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-owner-buyout-outsider-limit] See [partial changes of ownership](/q/partial-change-of-ownership).

This page covers the changes we can quote side by side. It isn't a full redline of each version; for that, compare the SOPs on SBA's site.

### How to stay current

- Read the current SOP on SBA's SOP 50 10 page, which lists each version and its effective date.[^sba-sop-50-10-versions]
- Watch for SBA Information Notices; SOP 50 10 8.1 itself was announced in one.[^sba-information-notice-5000-880695]
- Ask your lender, in writing, which version your loan is being underwritten under and whether a new one is expected before closing.
