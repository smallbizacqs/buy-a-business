**Short answer.** Owning a business can be lonelier and more stressful than a job, especially in the first year. Plan for it the way you'd plan for any other business risk: people outside the business you can talk to candidly, protected time for sleep, exercise and family, a team you trust with more of the load, and early attention when stress turns into something more. If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the United States, any time.

### Why owners feel isolated

As an owner you can't share every worry with employees, who depend on you; with the seller, who has moved on; or always with your family, who may have money at stake too. Decisions that end with you, such as letting someone go, a large customer leaving or a loan covenant, can feel heavy when there's no one at the same level to talk to. Personal guarantees on a loan add to that pressure. See [personal guarantees](/q/personal-guarantee).

### Signs to watch for

- Trouble sleeping, or thinking about the business all the time.
- Irritability with employees or family, or withdrawing from them.
- Dreading work, losing interest in things you used to enjoy.
- Drinking more, or other ways of numbing stress.
- Physical symptoms: headaches, stomach problems, frequent illness.
- Avoiding decisions, emails or the numbers.

These don't mean you're failing. They're signals to change something.

### What helps

- **People who understand.** A peer group of other owners, a mentor, or a friend who owns a business. See [boards and peer groups](/q/peer-groups-board).
- **Professional support.** A therapist or counselor, or a coach for the work side. Talking to a professional is a practical step, not a last resort. Your doctor is also a good place to start.
- **Structure for your time.** Set working hours and protect some time off each week. Working without breaks wears down judgment.
- **Delegation.** Each task you hand to someone capable reduces the load. See [making the business run without you](/q/professionalize-systems) and [hiring and strengthening the team](/q/hiring-team).
- **Visibility into the numbers.** Uncertainty feeds worry. A weekly cash report and a short scorecard replace vague fear with specific problems you can work on. See [reporting and key numbers](/q/financial-controls-kpis).
- **Basics.** Sleep, exercise and time with people outside work aren't luxuries for an owner; they keep your judgment intact.
- **Talk with your family** about the business's demands and risks, and agree how you'll protect time together.

### Before you buy

If you're still deciding, be realistic about the hours and pressure of the first years and talk it through with your partner or family. See [hours and lifestyle](/q/hours-lifestyle).

### If you need help now

If you are in crisis, or worried about someone else, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline offers counselors by call, text or chat, any time of day or night.[^lifeline-988-help] In an emergency, call 911.
