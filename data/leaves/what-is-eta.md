**Short answer.** Entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA) means becoming a business owner by buying an existing, profitable company and running it, rather than founding one. The path runs roughly: decide it suits you, choose how to fund it, define what you're looking for, find and approach owners, value the business and make an offer, check everything, close, then take over from the seller.

### Who does it

A person on this path is often called a **searcher**: someone looking for one business to buy and run. Some searchers pay their own way (a self-funded search); others raise money from investors to pay for the search (a search fund); some buy with partners or set up a holding company to own several businesses. See [choosing your path](/q/choose-path).

### The steps

1. **Decide.** Weigh buying against starting or franchising, and whether you have the money, temperament and family support. See [buy or start](/q/buy-vs-start) and [readiness](/q/readiness).
2. **Fund.** Line up your equity and learn how acquisition loans work. Many small deals use an SBA 7(a) loan, which a bank makes and the SBA partly guarantees.[^sba-7a-loans-page] Under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), an initial acquisition needs at least 10% buyer equity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-minimum] See [SBA 7(a) basics](/q/sba-7a-basics).
3. **Define what to buy.** Write a **buy box**: size, industry, geography and deal features you'll accept. See [defining a buy box](/q/define-buy-box).
4. **Find deals.** Through business brokers and listing sites, and by contacting owners whose businesses aren't for sale. See [how brokers work](/q/how-brokers-work) and [off-market sourcing](/q/off-market-sourcing).
5. **Size it up.** Work out real earnings, a fair price and what the business can borrow against. For an SBA loan, the lender tests debt coverage on historical results.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio] See [debt service coverage](/q/dscr).
6. **Make an offer.** A letter of intent (LOI) sets price, structure and an exclusivity period. See [what goes in an LOI](/q/loi-contents).
7. **Confirm.** Due diligence (checking the financials, contracts, employees and operations) runs alongside the lender's approval. See [deal killers](/q/deal-killers).
8. **Close.** Sign the purchase agreement and fund the deal. See [closing day](/q/closing-day).
9. **Take over.** The seller hands over relationships and know-how. In an SBA-financed initial acquisition the seller can stay only as a consultant, for up to 24 months.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-consultant] See [the seller's transition period](/q/seller-transition-period).

### How long it takes

It varies widely with your criteria, budget and market. Expect to look at many businesses for each one you make an offer on. See [search timeline](/q/search-timeline) and [deal funnel math](/q/deal-funnel-math).

### What it isn't

It isn't passive investing. The searcher usually becomes the chief executive and works in the business daily, at least at first. It also isn't a shortcut around risk: the buyer typically puts in savings and, with bank debt, signs a [personal guarantee](/q/personal-guarantee).
