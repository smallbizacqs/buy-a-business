**Short answer.** A finding should kill the deal when it means the business won't earn what you paid for, or exposes you to a risk you can't price or protect against. It should reprice or restructure the deal when the problem is real but measurable. Decide before diligence starts what would make you walk away, so you're not deciding under deal fatigue.

**Findings that often lead buyers to walk away:**

- Earnings don't hold up: bank deposits, tax returns and financial statements don't agree, and the seller can't explain why.
- Signs of fraud or deliberately misleading records.
- The business depends on something that won't transfer: the owner personally, a key customer, a license, or a lease the landlord won't assign.
- Large undisclosed liabilities, such as unpaid taxes, lawsuits or environmental problems.
- Revenue is falling and you don't have a credible reason to expect it to recover.

**Findings that more often lead to a new price or structure:** smaller earnings adjustments, a customer at risk, deferred maintenance or equipment that needs replacing, working capital lower than expected, or a known liability that can be covered by a specific indemnity or escrow.

**How SBA financing turns findings into numbers.** An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA). Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026):

- The lender must verify the seller's financial data against IRS records.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-tax-verification]
- When a quality of earnings (QoE) report (an accountant's independent check of whether the earnings are real and repeatable) is required, the lender calculates debt coverage from its findings and must reduce the loan if coverage doesn't support the deal.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-dsc]
- If the price exceeds the business valuation, the difference must be equity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-gap-equity]
- Total debt, including seller debt not on full standby, can't exceed the valuation.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-total-debt-cap]

If the deal no longer works at a price the seller will accept, that is the answer. See [when to walk away](/q/walk-away) and [changing the price after the LOI](/q/retrade).
