**Short answer.** Treat every amount owed to the seller as a contract with a calendar: put each obligation's dates and calculations in one schedule, pay or report on time, and keep records. If an SBA loan funded the deal, read the standby agreement before paying anything on a seller note. Under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), a note on full standby gets no principal or interest for the whole term of the 7(a) loan,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-debt-equity] and changing that is up to the lender, within SBA's rules.

### Build one schedule

From the purchase agreement, seller note, standby agreement and any side agreements, record each obligation's amount or formula, due date, and any notice or statement you owe. Typical items:

- **Seller note payments**: principal and interest by date, late fees, prepayment terms.
- **Consulting or transition pay.** In an SBA initial acquisition, the seller can stay on only as a consultant, for no more than 24 months in total.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-consultant] Pay for real services under the consulting agreement. See [seller consulting agreement](/q/seller-consulting-agreement).
- **Earnout or rebate statements**, if the deal has one.
- **Working-capital true-up and escrow releases.** These have their own deadlines; see [post-closing true-up](/q/post-closing-true-up).

**Tax reporting.** A business that pays at least $600 of interest in the course of its trade or business generally files Form 1099-INT for the recipient.[^irs-i1099int-business-interest] Ask your CPA how to report the seller-note interest and when.

### If the note is on standby (SBA deals)

- **What standby means now.** Under SOP 50 10 8.1, seller debt counts as equity only if it is subordinated and on full standby, with no principal or interest paid for the term of the 7(a) loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-debt-equity] Interest may accrue and be paid after the 7(a) loan is repaid. The seller subordinates its liens and takes no action against the business or collateral without the lender's consent.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-standby-terms]
- **When does standby end?** It depends on the rules your loan was approved under, and your standby agreement controls. Under SOP 50 10 7.1, seller debt could count as equity on full standby for the first 24 months, or on partial standby with interest-only payments.[^sba-sop-50-10-7-1-seller-standby] SOP 50 10 8 required full standby for the life of the loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-seller-standby] Check your agreement for the exact terms.
- **Changing the standby.** SBA's servicing rules tell lenders not to loosen a standby agreement to allow payments to the seller without a prudent basis. A standby that counted toward your equity injection can change only if the SOP in effect when the loan was approved allows it.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-standby-modification]
- **Don't pay around it.** Paying a standby seller outside the agreement, including "consulting" fees that are really note payments, breaks your commitment to the lender.

**A note that isn't on standby** can be paid during the loan, but it counts toward the cap on total acquisition debt and in the coverage test.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-total-debt-cap] **Refinancing it** with SBA financing requires the note to have been in place and current (not on standby) for at least 36 months after closing, and the refinance must improve the payment by at least 10%.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-note-refi-terms] See [when to refinance a seller note](/q/seller-note-refinance-timing).

### Earnouts and rebates

Under SOP 50 10 8.1, seller earnouts are prohibited in a 7(a) acquisition; buyer rebates based on business performance are allowed.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-earnouts] If the seller pays you a rebate, the money must go to pay down the 7(a) loan that funded the purchase,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-rebate-principal] and doing so doesn't trigger a subsidy recoupment fee.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-rebate-no-recoupment] See [buyer rebates](/q/buyer-rebate).

In a deal without SBA financing that has an earnout:

- Calculate exactly as the agreement defines the metric, using the accounting method it specifies.
- Deliver the statement and supporting numbers by the deadline, and calendar the seller's review period.
- Record decisions that affect the metric, such as pricing or accounting changes, with the business reason.

When a calculation is disputed, follow the process in the agreement; see [earnout disputes](/q/earnout-disputes).

### When you think the seller owes you

If you have a claim against the seller, for example under an indemnity, don't simply stop paying the note. Whether you can offset depends on the documents, and a standby or subordination agreement may limit what you can do. Talk to your lawyer first. See [set-off and default](/q/set-off-default).
