**Short answer.** It varies by lender and by how ready your deal is, so ask each lender how long its recent acquisition loans took from complete package to approval, and from approval to closing. The biggest factors are whether the lender can approve the loan under its own delegated SBA authority, how quickly the valuation and any quality of earnings report (an accountant's independent check of whether the earnings are real and repeatable) come back, and how fast you, the seller and third parties such as the landlord respond. An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

**Two approval routes.** Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026):

- **Delegated (PLP) lenders** decide the loan under their own SBA authority. They may finish the business valuation and quality of earnings (QoE) report after the SBA loan number is issued, as long as the work is under way and done before closing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-plp-valuation-timing]
- **Non-delegated loans** go to the SBA's processing center, where SBA makes the final decision on eligibility and creditworthiness and approves or declines the loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lgpc-decision]

**Steps that take time:**

1. Your application and the lender's underwriting.
2. The business valuation and, if required, the QoE.
3. IRS tax transcripts. The lender requests them with Form 4506-C or 8821, and must follow up if the IRS hasn't answered within 10 business days.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-transcript-follow-up]
4. Approval, then the lender's closing conditions. Every condition in the credit memo, such as the equity injection, standby agreements and appraisals, must be met before or at disbursement.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-conditions-before-disbursement]
5. Closing. A lender may hold loan funds in escrow for no more than 5 business days to facilitate closing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-closing-escrow]

**Questions to ask a lender:** Do you have delegated SBA authority, and will you use it for this loan? How long did your last few acquisition loans take at each step? Which valuation and QoE providers do you use, and how long are they taking now? What makes your deals slip?

Line up your exclusivity period with the answers. See [exclusivity](/q/exclusivity) and [what the lender needs](/q/lender-package).
