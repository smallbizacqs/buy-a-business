**Short answer.** Start with three must-haves: what the law requires (which varies by state), what your lender requires, and what your lease and customer contracts require. Then insure the losses the business couldn't absorb on its own. With an SBA 7(a) loan, the lender's requirements can include hazard insurance on the collateral, flood insurance if a pledged building is in a flood zone, and sometimes life insurance on you. Once you own the business, the job is keeping all of it in force, with the lender named correctly, through every renewal.

### Required by law

SBA's business guide lists workers' compensation, unemployment and disability insurance as required for businesses with employees.[^sba-business-insurance-employees] The insurance a business must carry varies by state,[^sba-business-insurance-required] so check your state's requirements, and those of any other state where you have employees or vehicles.

### Required by your lender (SBA 7(a))

Under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026):

- **Hazard insurance.** Required on all collateral for 7(a) loans over $50,000.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-hazard-insurance] Coverage must be for full replacement cost and name the lender in a loss payable clause. The policy must give the lender at least 10 days' notice before cancellation. Where the state requires added coverage such as wind, hail or earthquake, you need a separate policy for it.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-hazard-coverage-terms] An SBA Express lender may skip hazard insurance on a small loan if it documents why.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-hazard-express-exception]
- **Flood insurance.** The requirement turns on FEMA's flood hazard determination.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-flood-insurance] If any part of a building pledged as collateral is in a special flood hazard area, you must carry flood insurance on it.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-flood-sfha] Required flood coverage must stay in place for the life of the loan. If a gap isn't fixed within 45 days, the lender buys the coverage at your expense.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-flood-life-of-loan]
- **Life insurance.** On a Standard 7(a) loan that isn't fully secured, life insurance equal to the collateral shortfall is required on the owner of a business that depends on one person.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-life-insurance-shortfall] For 7(a) Small and Express loans, the lender's own policy decides.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-small-express-life-insurance] A required policy is assigned to the lender as collateral, and you pay the premiums. An existing policy can be pledged, and credit life or whole life should not be required.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-life-insurance-assignment] If the insured dies, the proceeds generally pay down the loan.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-life-insurance-proceeds]
- **Liability.** The lender's credit memo for an acquisition addresses business hazard and liability insurance,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-insurance-credit-memo] so ask whether your approval sets liability limits.

After closing, the lender must check that required insurance stays in force for the whole loan term.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-insurance-monitoring] If required hazard coverage lapses, the lender may buy it itself (force-place it) and recover the cost.[^sba-sop-50-57-4-force-placed-insurance] See [what the lender expects after closing](/q/lender-after-closing).

### Required by contracts

Your lease, customer contracts, licenses and any franchise agreement may set minimum limits, require that a party be named as additional insured, or require notice before a policy changes. Make a list of every such requirement during the first weeks and check your policies against it.

### Coverage to consider beyond the requirements

SBA describes general liability insurance as coverage for any business and professional liability insurance (errors and omissions) as coverage for businesses that provide services.[^sba-business-insurance-types] Depending on the business, also price:

- Commercial property, and business interruption (lost income while you recover from damage)
- Commercial auto, if the business owns or uses vehicles
- Cyber, if you hold customer data or take payments online
- Employment practices liability
- An umbrella policy over liability and auto
- Key person coverage for an employee the business depends on, and disability coverage for yourself

What you need, and at what limits, depends on what could go wrong and what you could pay from cash. Get quotes from more than one commercial broker.

### Keeping it current

- Put every renewal date on a calendar and send updated certificates to the lender and landlord before the old ones expire.
- Confirm that lender clauses and additional insured endorsements carry over at each renewal.
- Tell your insurer when you add vehicles, locations, employees or services.

Reviewing the seller's insurance during diligence and binding coverage for closing day is covered in [insurance review](/q/insurance-review). Insurance against a personal guarantee is covered in [personal guarantee insurance](/q/pg-insurance).
