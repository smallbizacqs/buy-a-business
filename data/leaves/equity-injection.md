**Short answer.** Under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), buying a business with an SBA 7(a) loan as an initial acquisition requires an equity injection (your own contribution) of at least 10%, which the lender can't reduce or waive.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-minimum] It's measured against the total project cost, not just the price.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-project-cost] Unborrowed cash counts in full; a full-standby seller note and some other sources can cover no more than half.

**What the 10% is measured against**

Total project cost means all costs required to become operational, whatever the source of funds, except lines of credit and 504 loans.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-project-cost] Closing costs, working capital and fees financed in the project raise the base.

**Sources that can supply all of it**

- Cash that isn't borrowed, from the business's balance sheet or elsewhere, including a gift.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-unlimited-sources]
- A personal loan to a guarantor if it's repaid from a source other than the business's cash flow; your salary from the business doesn't count.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-unlimited-sources]
- Money you spend on required financial due diligence reports (the valuation and any quality of earnings report).[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-diligence-costs]

**Sources capped at half**

Limited sources, alone or together, can supply no more than half of the required equity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-limited-sources] They include a seller note that is subordinated and on full standby, with no principal or interest paid for the life of the 7(a) loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-debt-equity] See [seller note as equity](/q/seller-note-as-equity).

**What doesn't count**

Fees for education, advisory services, or an agent.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-prepaid-not-equity]

**When you need more than 10%**

If the price is above the lender's business valuation, the difference must be paid with equity.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-valuation-gap-equity] Coverage can also force more equity: a smaller loan means smaller payments. See [DSCR](/q/dscr).

Lenders can ask for more than the minimum, and the SBA's rules change; confirm with your lender and the current SOP.
