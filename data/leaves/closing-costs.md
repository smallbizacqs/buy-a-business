**Short answer.** There's no standard total; it depends on the loan, the deal's size and complexity, and the advisors you use. The costs fall into a few groups: lender and SBA fees, third-party reports, your own advisors, closing and filing costs, and the setup costs of taking over. Ask your lender early for an itemized estimate of its fees and pass-through costs, get written fee quotes from each advisor, and add them up yourself, because together they raise how much cash you need.

### Lender and SBA fees

- **SBA guaranty fee (upfront fee).** For a 7(a) loan, the lender pays SBA an upfront fee for the guaranty and may pass the cost to you. It's calculated on the guaranteed portion of the loan, not the total loan amount, and SBA can change it for a fiscal year by Information Notice.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-upfront-fee] You can pay it from loan proceeds.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-upfront-fee-proceeds] Because the rate changes, ask your lender for the current figure on your loan amount. (SOP 50 10 8.1, effective October 1, 2026.)
- **Packaging fees.** The lender may charge reasonable, customary fees for packaging the application.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-packaging-fees]
- **Loan agents.** SBA doesn't require you to use a packager or referral agent, and lenders and agents must tell you so.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-agent-not-required] If one is paid, the fee is disclosed on SBA Form 159.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-form-159]
- **The lender's legal fees.** Legal costs the lender passes on to you must be charged by the hour.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-lender-legal-hourly]
- **Out-of-pocket costs.** The lender may pass on filing and recording fees, collateral appraisals, environmental reports and other direct closing charges.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-out-of-pocket]

### Reports and diligence

- Business valuation, and a quality of earnings report if required or wanted. Their cost may be passed to you, and what you spend on them can count toward your equity injection.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-diligence-costs] See [QoE cost and scope](/q/qoe-cost-scope).
- Equipment or real estate appraisals, environmental reports, and lien, litigation and other public-record searches.

### Your own advisors

- **Lawyer** for the purchase agreement, ancillary documents, lease and consents. See [choosing counsel](/q/choosing-counsel).
- **CPA** for financial and tax diligence, structure and the purchase price allocation.
- **Others** as needed: an insurance broker, an industry consultant, a background check provider.

Ask each for a fixed fee or a capped estimate, and what's not included.

### Closing and filing costs

- Escrow or closing agent fees, if one is used.
- Filing fees to form your acquiring company, register it in other states, and record lien releases and new security filings.
- Transfer fees: franchise transfer fees, license and permit transfer or application fees, and any landlord assignment fee. See [franchise resales](/q/franchise-transfer) and [licenses and permits](/q/licenses-permits-transfer).
- Transfer or sales taxes on assets in some states, such as on vehicles or equipment; ask your CPA.

### Setup costs

- Insurance premiums and deposits due at closing. See [insurance](/q/insurance-review).
- Payroll, bank, merchant processing and software setup.
- Working capital, if you're funding it separately.

### Who pays what

Some costs are clearly yours (your lawyer, your loan fees). Others are negotiable: escrow fees, transfer fees, the seller's payoffs and lien releases, and franchise fees. Settle these in the LOI or the purchase agreement, not on closing day. If a broker represents the seller, the seller's listing agreement sets the commission; confirm in writing that it comes out of the seller's proceeds and not from you.

### Why the total matters for financing

With an SBA loan, your equity injection is measured against total project cost, meaning all costs required to become operational, not just the price.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-equity-project-cost] Financing closing costs into the loan raises the base, and so raises the equity you need. See [equity injection](/q/equity-injection), [cash at close](/q/cash-at-close) and [what happens on closing day](/q/closing-day).
