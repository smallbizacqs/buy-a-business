**Short answer.** Get a business under LOI first, then take a short, specific package to people who already know and trust you or invest in small acquisitions: individuals, family offices and funds that back independent sponsors. Expect them to judge you, the business and the terms, in that order. Before you send anything, decide whether you're raising under Rule 506(b), which bars general solicitation (public advertising),[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-502-general-solicitation] or 506(c), which allows it but limits you to verified accredited investors.[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-506-c-accredited][^ecfr-17-cfr-230-506-c-verification]

### Who invests in single deals

- **People you know** with the means and appetite for a private, illiquid investment: former colleagues, business owners, executives. Depending on the exemption, they may need to be accredited investors, for example with net worth over $1 million excluding their home, or income over $200,000 ($300,000 joint).[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-501-accredited-net-worth][^ecfr-17-cfr-230-501-accredited-income] See [who counts as accredited](/q/accredited-investor).
- **Search fund and small-business investors** who back searchers and independent sponsors repeatedly.
- **Family offices**. See [family office capital](/q/family-office-capital).
- **Funds that co-invest** with independent sponsors.

### The process

1. **Line up a short list before you have a deal.** Tell potential investors what you're looking for and ask whether they'd want to see a deal. Under 506(b), you can't find them through general solicitation.[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-502-general-solicitation]
2. **Sign the LOI** with enough time in exclusivity to raise. See [exclusivity](/q/exclusivity).
3. **Send a teaser, then the full package** to interested investors.
4. **Hold calls and answer diligence questions.** Share the quality of earnings report and lender term sheet when you have them.
5. **Collect soft commitments, then signed subscription documents.** Have a backup plan for a shortfall.
6. **Close the raise with the acquisition**, and file Form D within 15 days of the first sale.[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-503-form-d]

### The package

- A one-page summary: the business, the price, the capital structure and what you're asking for.
- An investment memo: why this business, why now, why you, the risks and how you'll handle them.
- A financial model with sources and uses, debt terms, base and downside cases, and returns. See [build a deal model](/q/deal-model).
- The proposed terms: securities offered, preferred return, your promote and fees, governance. See [investor terms](/q/investor-terms).
- Your background and references.

### Rules to plan around

- **506(b) or 506(c).** 506(b) allows up to 35 non-accredited but sophisticated purchasers in a 90-day period[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-506-b-purchasers] and bars general solicitation;[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-502-general-solicitation] 506(c) allows advertising but every buyer must be accredited and verified.[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-506-c-accredited][^ecfr-17-cfr-230-506-c-verification] The rule's examples of general solicitation include any advertisement, article or notice published in media,[^ecfr-17-cfr-230-502-examples] so think before you post about a raise. See [Rule 506(b) or 506(c)](/q/506b-506c-securities).
- **Paying finders.** A "broker" is anyone in the business of effecting securities transactions for others.[^usc-15-78c-broker-definition] Paying a commission to someone who brings investors can raise registration issues; ask securities counsel first.
- **SBA loans.** Every investor, direct or indirect, must meet SBA's citizenship test;[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-citizenship-owners] owners of 20% or more must guarantee;[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-personal-guaranty] and investor money used for the equity injection can receive only tax distributions until the loan is repaid.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-investor-distributions] See [SBA rules for investors](/q/investor-equity-rules).

For the offering documents, buyers who hire counsel can look for AI-using law firms with fixed fees that are lean and run by an attorney with big-law experience.
