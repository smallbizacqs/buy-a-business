**Short answer.** You can usually search while employed; it's slower, and you'll need to answer brokers and owners promptly in limited hours. Owning while keeping a full-time job is harder: an SBA lender wants to know who will run the business day to day, and you'll need a capable manager in place. One approach is to keep your job until a deal is real, then leave around the lender's commitment or closing.

### Searching while employed

- **Time.** Reviewing listings, calls with brokers and owners, and early analysis can fit into evenings and some daytime calls. Site visits and diligence need more.
- **Speed matters.** A good business may attract several buyers, and slow replies can cost you the deal. See [competition](/q/competition-crowded).
- **Check your employment terms.** Read your employment agreement and company policy on outside activities, confidentiality and non-competes before you start. Use personal devices and time for the search.
- **Budget.** A paycheck reduces the pressure to buy the wrong business just to stop spending savings. See [search budget](/q/search-budget).

### Owning while employed

- **Who runs it.** For an SBA 7(a) loan, under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), the lender's credit memo describes the principals' involvement in daily onsite management, or how daily operations will be managed if they aren't there daily.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-management-experience] Expect questions about the manager's experience and pay.
- **No hands-off structures.** A business that hands a third-party management company sole discretion over its operations is an ineligible passive business for SBA lending.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-passive-management-agreement]
- **Your pay.** The lender checks that what you draw from the business covers your obligations and living expenses;[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-owner-compensation] outside income changes that picture. Ask how your lender treats it.
- **The economics.** A manager's salary comes out of the cash flow you'd otherwise keep or use for debt. See [running it yourself or hiring a manager](/q/operator-vs-absentee).

### When to quit

Points buyers use:

- **Signed LOI.** You have a deal but no financing yet.
- **Lender commitment.** Financing is likely; diligence continues.
- **Closing.** The least risky moment to leave, if you can handle diligence and closing while working.

Weigh your household's runway, health insurance, any bonus or vesting you'd give up, and how much time the deal needs from you before closing. See [readiness](/q/readiness).
