**Short answer.** Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, effective October 1, 2026, an SBA 7(a) lender must get a quality of earnings (QoE) report (an accountant's independent check of whether the earnings are real and repeatable) for an initial acquisition or business expansion when the business purchase price is $3 million or more. The $3 million is measured on the price before any buyer equity, seller debt or other financing. Owner buyouts and ESOP (employee stock ownership plan) or cooperative deals are exempt. An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

**The rule.** For business expansion and initial acquisition transactions with a purchase price of $3 million or more, the lender must obtain a QoE in addition to the required business valuation, and the threshold is set before the application of buyer equity, seller debt or other financing sources.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-threshold] SOP 50 10 8.1 applies to loans given an SBA loan number on or after October 1, 2026.[^sba-information-notice-5000-880695]

**What counts toward the $3 million.** The SBA's "Business Purchase Price" excludes owner-occupied commercial real estate bought in the deal.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-business-purchase-price]

**Exceptions:**

- Owner buyouts and ESOP or cooperative transactions aren't subject to the QoE requirement.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-owner-buyout-exempt]
- No QoE is required when the deal involves an owner-occupied special purpose property (a building designed for one use), whatever the price.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-special-purpose]

**What the lender does with it.** The lender must calculate debt coverage from the QoE's findings, and if coverage doesn't support the valuation and debt structure, the loan must be reduced.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-dsc] A lender with delegated authority may finish the QoE after the SBA loan number is issued, if it is under way and done before closing.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-plp-valuation-timing]

SBA rules change. Check the current SOP and ask your lender how it applies to your deal. See [do I need a QoE?](/q/qoe-need) and [QoE cost and scope](/q/qoe-cost-scope).
