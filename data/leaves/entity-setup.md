**Short answer.** Buyers commonly buy through a company they form for the purpose, usually a limited liability company (LLC) or a corporation, rather than in their own name, so the business's debts and lawsuits are kept apart from personal assets as far as the law allows. Which type, and how it's taxed, depends on your plans, your state and your taxes, so decide it with your CPA and lawyer before the purchase agreement is drafted, since the buyer named in it is that company.

**The basic choices.** Your form of business determines which tax return it files. The common forms are sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation and S corporation, and an LLC is a structure allowed by state law.[^irs-business-structures] Your CPA can explain how each option would be taxed in your situation.

**Some things to decide:**

- **One company or a holding company.** Some buyers form a holding company that owns the operating company, to add businesses later. That adds cost and paperwork.
- **Partners and investors.** If anyone else is putting in money, the operating agreement or shareholder agreement sets out who decides what and who gets paid when.
- **State.** Forming in the state where the business operates is simplest; forming elsewhere can add registration steps there.

**With an SBA 7(a) loan,** the structure affects who borrows. Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), in an asset purchase the acquiring company is the borrower;[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-asset-purchase-borrower] when a new owner buys 100% of the shares, the business and the buyer are co-borrowers.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-borrower-structure] Ask your lender before you form anything. An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

**Practical steps:** form the company, get its employer identification number (EIN), and open its bank account before closing. If your company takes over another employer's business, it doesn't use the seller's EIN.[^irs-pub-15-successor-ein] See [day-one readiness](/q/day-one-readiness).
