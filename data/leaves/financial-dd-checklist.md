**Short answer.** A financial diligence request list asks for the records you need to prove the earnings, the cash and the balance sheet: three years of tax returns and financial statements plus the year to date, bank statements, the general ledger, customer-level sales, payroll records, a debt schedule, receivables and payables agings, inventory counts, and the documents behind every add-back. Ask only for what you'll actually use, and say why when an item is unusual.

### Earnings

- **Tax returns** for the last three years, and permission for transcripts. In an SBA-financed change of ownership the lender must verify the seller's financial data against IRS records,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-tax-verification] using IRS Form 4506-C or 8821 to request tax transcripts.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-transcript-follow-up]
- **Profit and loss statements and balance sheets** for the same three years and year to date, monthly if they exist. An SBA lender's analysis of an acquired business is based on the three most recent year-ends,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-three-years] and it compares the latest interim statement with the same period a year earlier.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-interim-statements]
- **General ledger detail** for the same period, exported from the accounting system, so you can trace individual entries.
- **The add-back schedule** with support for each item: receipts, payroll records for family members, one-time invoices. See [add-backs](/q/add-backs).

### Cash

- **Bank and credit card statements** for every account, for the same months as the financial statements. These are the raw material for a [proof of cash](/q/proof-of-cash). An SBA-required quality of earnings report includes a cash proof that reconciles bank statements to the income statement and tax returns for the trailing 12 months and the last two fiscal years.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-cash-proof]
- **Merchant processor statements** for card-heavy businesses, and deposit records for cash-heavy ones.

### Revenue

- **Sales by customer, by month,** as a system export, for the same period. See [revenue quality and churn](/q/revenue-quality-churn).
- **Contracts** with the largest customers, and any price lists or rate cards.
- **Backlog, deposits and deferred revenue**: work customers have paid for that isn't done yet.

### Costs and people

- **Payroll registers** and payroll tax filings, with a roster showing roles, pay, start dates and whether each person is an employee or a contractor. See [employee diligence](/q/employee-dd).
- **Vendor list** with annual spend, and any supply contracts or rebates.
- **Rent, insurance and other fixed costs**, with the lease and policies behind them.

### Balance sheet

- **Debt schedule**: every loan, line of credit, equipment financing, merchant cash advance and seller obligation, with balances and payoff terms.
- **Receivables and payables agings** (lists of unpaid customer and vendor invoices by how old they are).
- **Inventory** listing, count method and any write-offs. See [receivables, payables and inventory](/q/ar-ap-inventory).
- **Fixed asset list** with age and condition, and recent and planned equipment purchases.
- **Working capital history**, so you can set a target level for closing. See [net working capital](/q/what-is-nwc) and the [peg](/q/peg). An SBA lender also analyzes whether working capital is adequate for at least the next 12 months.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-working-capital-adequacy]

### Taxes

- Sales and use tax returns, payroll tax returns and any notices from tax agencies. See [tax diligence](/q/tax-dd).

### Keeping the list useful

- **Don't duplicate the lender.** An SBA application to buy a business already includes the purchase agreement, the valuation, a pro forma balance sheet and three years of the seller's financial statements.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-application-business-purchase] Coordinate so the seller sends each document once. See [the lender package](/q/lender-package).
- **Build on what you already have.** If you received documents before the LOI, list them and ask only for what's new. See [early document requests](/q/early-docs-request).
- **Track it.** Keep one tracker with each item, date requested, date received and open questions, and share it with the seller and your advisors.
