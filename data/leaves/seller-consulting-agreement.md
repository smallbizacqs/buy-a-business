**Short answer.** Put the seller's help after closing in a separate written agreement that says what they'll do, for how long, how many hours, how they're paid, and how it ends. Keep it tied to real work, such as introducing customers, training staff and handing over know-how. If you use an SBA 7(a) loan (a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration) for an initial acquisition, the seller can only be a consultant, not an employee or officer, for up to 24 months in total.

**SBA rule.** Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), in an initial acquisition or business expansion the seller may not remain as an officer, director, stockholder or employee. If a transition period is needed, the business may contract with the seller as a consultant for no more than 24 months, including any extensions.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-seller-consultant]

**What the agreement usually covers:**

- **Scope**: specific handovers: customer and vendor introductions, licenses, pricing, systems, how jobs are run.
- **Time**: hours per week or month, often tapering, and whether on site or remote.
- **Term and ending it**: the length, and what lets either side end it early.
- **Pay**: hourly, monthly or fixed, and when it's paid. Payments here are for services, which your CPA and the seller's may treat differently for tax than purchase price, so don't use the agreement to move price around.
- **Authority**: the seller advises; you decide. Say whether the seller can speak for the business.
- **Confidentiality and non-solicitation** during and after the term.

See [how long the seller should stay](/q/seller-transition-period) and [a seller who won't let go](/q/seller-wont-let-go).

## What practitioners say
- Barlow & Williams describe a deal where the seller refused a seller note and the buyer proposed a three-year consulting agreement instead.[^c453de6] A three-year term would be longer than the 24-month consultant limit in an SBA-financed initial acquisition, so check how your financing affects a structure like this.
