**Short answer.** Some do and many don't. Whether a license or permit can move to you depends on the agency that issued it, the type of license, and whether you're buying assets or the company itself. Make a list of every license the business holds, ask each issuing agency what a new owner must do, and build the time that takes into your closing date.

**Start with the list.** Most small businesses need a mix of federal and state licenses and permits, and federally regulated activities need a federal license.[^sba-licenses-permits-federal] Which state, county and city licenses apply depends on the business's activities and location.[^sba-licenses-permits] Ask the seller for every license, permit and registration, with the issuing agency, number, holder and expiry date.

**Three common situations:**

- **Held by the company.** In a stock purchase, the company keeps holding it, but the agency may require notice or approval of the change of ownership. Check each one.
- **Held by the company, asset purchase.** Your new company usually has to apply in its own name, or the agency has to approve a transfer.
- **Held by a person.** Some licenses belong to an individual, such as a professional or contractor license held by the owner or a qualifying employee. If that person leaves, the business may not be able to keep operating. Find out who that is and plan for it.

**Licenses that can be transferred** can carry value, and lenders care. In an SBA 7(a) change of ownership, the lender must take all assets conveyed, including transferable licenses such as a liquor license, as collateral (SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, effective October 1, 2026).[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-transferable-licenses] An SBA 7(a) loan is a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

**In the purchase agreement:** make the licenses you need to operate a closing condition, and have the seller cooperate with the transfer. If an approval comes only after closing, that may mean signing and closing on different days. See [closing day](/q/closing-day).
