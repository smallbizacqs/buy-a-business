**Short answer.** On the first day you own the business, you need to be able to take payments, pay people, and be insured, without a gap. That means bank and payment accounts in your company's name, payroll set up for the first pay date, insurance bound from the closing date, and access to every system the business runs on. Plan these with the seller weeks before closing.

**Money**

- Your company's bank account, with online access and the right people authorized.
- Card processing, online payments and any customer autopay moved or ready to move, so customer payments land in your account.
- The seller's accounts closed or swept, with a plan for payments that still arrive in them.

**People and payroll**

- Payroll provider set up, with the first pay date confirmed and employees told.
- Your company uses its own employer identification number (EIN), not the seller's.[^irs-pub-15-successor-ein]
- New Forms W-4 from transferred employees, unless an IRS alternative procedure applies.[^irs-pub-15-successor-w4]
- A decision on Forms I-9: complete new ones for all acquired employees, or keep the seller's and take on their errors.[^uscis-m274-acquisitions]
- Benefits continued without a gap.

**Insurance.** Policies in your company's name starting at closing. With an SBA 7(a) loan (a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration), SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026) has the lender's credit memo addresses life insurance (on whom, how much, or why none) and business hazard and liability insurance,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-insurance-credit-memo] so your lender will tell you what it needs to see at closing.

**Access and operations**

- Passwords for email, the website, domain, software, phone numbers and social accounts, moved to you.
- Vendor and supplier accounts in your company's name.
- Licenses and permits in place for the new owner. See [licenses and permits](/q/licenses-permits-transfer).
- Keys, alarm codes and utilities.

See [closing day](/q/closing-day) and [your first 100 days](/q/first-100-days-plan).
