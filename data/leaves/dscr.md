**Short answer.** The debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is the business's cash flow divided by its total annual loan payments. For an SBA 7(a) loan to buy a business, under SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026), the lender must see at least 1.25:1 for an initial acquisition or owner buyout and 1.15:1 for a business expansion, measured on historical results.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio]

**How the SBA defines it**

Historical DSC is EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) divided by the combined debt service after the transaction, meaning every loan payment, not just the SBA loan.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-definition] The lender tests either the last fiscal year-end or the average of the last two.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-dsc-ratio]

**What goes into the numbers**

- **Adjustments.** The lender may make prudent adjustments for savings the transaction will realize,[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-adjustments] but must explain why each one is prudent, necessary and supported by ongoing operations.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-adjustment-justification]
- **No forecasts.** The lender must look at your projections but can't rely on them to meet the ratio, except for an owner-occupied special purpose property.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-projections]
- **Seller notes and other debt.** Interest-only acquisition debt that isn't on full standby is counted as if it amortized over no more than 10 years.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-interest-only-debt]
- **Your personal finances.** A global cash flow analysis, which adds in affiliated businesses, must meet 1:1.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-global-cash-flow]
- **A quality of earnings report.** When one is required, the lender calculates coverage from its findings and must reduce the loan if coverage doesn't support the deal.[^sba-sop-50-10-8-1-qoe-dsc]

**Working it the other way**

Divide the cash flow the lender accepts by the minimum ratio to get the most annual debt service the deal can carry. Everything above that has to come from equity or from debt that isn't paid during the loan (full standby).
