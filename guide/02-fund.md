This stage is for choosing how you'll search and how you'll pay. You're here once you've decided to buy and need to pick a path (self-funded, investor-backed search fund, independent sponsor or holding company) and understand the money: an SBA 7(a) loan (a bank loan partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration), seller financing, your own equity and any investors. SBA rules below refer to SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective October 1, 2026); they change often, so check the current version.
