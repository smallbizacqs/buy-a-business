This stage is for deciding what you'll buy and finding businesses that fit. You're here once you know roughly how you'll pay and need to set your criteria (your "buy box"), work with brokers and marketplaces, reach owners whose businesses aren't listed, and plan how long and how much the search will take.

## Your buy box

A buy box is your written list of criteria: size, industry, location, earnings quality and your role. It lets you say no quickly and tells brokers what to send you.

- [How do I define my buy box?](/q/define-buy-box)

- [How do I pick an industry?](/q/industry-selection)
- [Why look for recurring revenue, and which businesses have it?](/q/recurring-revenue)
- [What makes a small business worth buying?](/q/what-makes-a-good-target)
- [Should I buy locally or move for the right deal?](/q/geography-relocation)

## Brokers and marketplaces

Brokers usually represent sellers, and marketplaces publish listings without checking them. Know whose side each party is on and what each marketplace shows before you rely on a listing.

## Finding owners who aren't listed

Many businesses are never listed. Buyers reach owners directly by email, phone and letters, through lists and data sources, and through accountants, attorneys and bankers who know owners thinking about selling.

A search is a funnel: many businesses reviewed for each offer, and not every signed offer closes. Plan your timeline and living costs with that in mind.
