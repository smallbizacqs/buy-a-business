This stage is for your first conversations with owners and brokers and the early screen before you make an offer. You're here once you have businesses to talk to: you need to open conversations well, earn an owner's trust, look credible to brokers, sign a nondisclosure agreement (NDA), read the seller's documents, and decide whether a business is worth an offer.

## Talking to owners

First calls and meetings are about understanding the owner and the business, not price. Owners often care about their employees, customers and legacy as much as the number, and many aren't ready the first time you call.

- [What do I say on the first call with an owner?](/q/first-conversation)
- [How should I run the first meeting with an owner?](/q/first-meeting)
- [What do I do when an owner isn't ready to sell yet?](/q/when-owner-not-ready)
- [How do I earn a retiring owner's trust?](/q/earn-trust)
- [What do sellers care about besides price?](/q/seller-psychology)
- [How do I find out why the owner is really selling?](/q/why-owner-selling)
- [How do I win a deal without the highest offer?](/q/buyer-of-choice)

## Working with brokers

Brokers work for the seller and prioritize buyers who look likely to close. Show how you'll pay, what you're looking for and why this business fits.

- [How do I look credible to brokers?](/q/broker-credibility)
- [How do I get a deal done with an unresponsive or difficult broker?](/q/bad-brokers)

Skill: `business-broker-relations`

## The early screen

Before an offer, read the teaser and the confidential information memorandum (CIM) critically, ask for the core financial documents, and pass early on problems that can't be fixed with price or terms.

- [What should I know before signing an NDA with a seller?](/q/nda)
- [How do I read a teaser or CIM, and what's missing from it?](/q/cim-teaser)
- [Which early red flags should make me pass before an offer?](/q/early-red-flags)
- [How much checking should I do before making an offer?](/q/pre-loi-diligence)
- [Which documents should I get before making an offer?](/q/early-docs-request)
