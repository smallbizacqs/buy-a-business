This stage is for deciding whether buying a small business is right for you before you spend money looking. You're here if you're weighing buying against starting something or staying in a job, wondering what an owner actually earns and does all week, or trying to understand what you'd be putting at risk. The answers depend on your savings, your tolerance for risk (including a personal guarantee on a loan), whether you'd run the business yourself, and your timeline.

## Buy, start or franchise

Buying gets you customers, staff and cash flow on day one, at the price of paying for them and inheriting how the business already works. Starting from scratch costs less up front but takes longer to produce income. Franchises sit in between, with a brand and system you pay for. Entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA) is the name for buying a business as a career path.
