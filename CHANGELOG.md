# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-27)

First public release.

- A guide to buying a small business in ten stages, 43 topics and 249 questions, each answered with dated primary sources (383 verified quotes from the SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, IRS, SEC, eCFR, DOL, FTC and others).
- 46 agent skills: one per topic, plus `buy-a-business` (start here) and `grill-my-deal` (stress-test a deal before you offer, sign or close). Ten stage packs for the Claude plugin marketplace.
- Skills are self-contained when installed: each ships its workspace spec and the sources it cites, with re-check dates.
- A shared workspace (`workspace/SPEC.md`) so work carries over between sessions and skills.
- Tested scripts for the arithmetic: debt service coverage and affordability, proof of cash, add-backs, valuation ranges, working-capital peg, deal model, seller-note structures, diligence calendars, lender timelines.
- Practitioner clips tied to the questions they speak to, shown as views, never as facts.
